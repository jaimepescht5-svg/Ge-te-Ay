# 29 — Wardrobe & Character Customization

> How players express the three leads through appearance — and how the systems
> tie clothing to **stealth, Heat, disguise, and story** rather than treating it
> as pure cosmetics. Identity you can wear ([doc 03](03-characters.md),
> [doc 15](15-art-direction.md)).

## Philosophy

- **Costume is character.** Each lead has a **silhouette and signature** that must
  survive any outfit — players personalize *within* an identity, they don't erase
  it ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)).
- **Clothes do something.** Concealment, disguise, weather, and faction-reading
  are mechanical, so the wardrobe is a tool, not just a dress-up screen
  ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)).
- **The city's fashion is the melting pot** — warm-climate, multicultural,
  loud-and-faded; the closet should feel like Port Soleil, not a mall.

## Per-lead signature looks (the anchors)

| Lead | Silhouette anchor | Default vibe | Wardrobe range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rae** | Driver's jacket, boots, tied-back hair | Practical, worn-in, controlled | Workwear ↔ understated sharp |
| **Theo** | Hood/layers, hands-in-pockets, low profile | Cold, deliberate, forgettable-on-purpose | Streetwear ↔ tactical ↔ "invisible" |
| **Frankie** | Loud shirt, gold, always slightly too much | Flashy, warm, trying too hard (lovably) | Party ↔ hustler-formal ↔ disguise comedy |

> **Rule:** you can dress Frankie down and Theo up, but the *read* at distance
> stays theirs — a costume that breaks the silhouette is a costume we cut.

## Customization layers

- **Body & grooming:** hair, facial hair, makeup, tattoos, accessories — within
  each lead's identity (we're personalizing a defined character, not a blank
  creator avatar; their faces are fixed and story-canon).
- **Clothing slots:** head, eyewear, top, outerwear, bottoms, footwear, gloves,
  accessories/jewelry, watches.
- **Condition & weathering:** salt-worn, sweat-stained, fresh-pressed — fits the
  art identity ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)) and can signal arc state.
- **Outfit presets:** save loadout-linked fits ("the heist quiet kit," "the gala
  fit," "the swamp kit") for one-tap swaps.

## Clothing as a system (the part that matters)

### Concealment & Heat
- Outfits have a **concealment profile** — baggy layers hide more weapons; a tailored
  suit hides little. Walking armed-and-obvious raises Heat
  ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)).
- **Color & flash** affect how memorable you are to witnesses — a neon shirt makes
  a worse getaway than gray.

### Disguises (heist tool)
- **Role outfits** (catering staff, dock worker, security, congregation, valet)
  let you pass in restricted zones until you act suspicious or get scanned
  ([doc 07](07-missions.md), [doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)). Disguise integrity is
  a stealth resource.

### Weather & district fit
- **Climate matters:** heavy gear in the noon heat draws looks; the storm rewards
  rain-appropriate kit. **District dress codes** — show up to the Heights in swamp
  boots and security clocks you faster ([doc 06](06-map-districts.md)).

### Faction colors
- Wearing a faction's colors shifts how their members and rivals read you —
  useful, and dangerous in the wrong territory ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)).

## Acquisition & economy

- **Shops by district** (Suncoast Outfitters, boutiques in the Heights, thrift in
  Sabal, the Cut's markets) — geography flavors the closet.
- **Earned & story fits** — unique pieces from arcs and Strangers
  ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)); the gala tux, the restored-garage jacket.
- **Lifestyle spending sink** ([doc 10](10-economy-progression.md)) — fashion is a
  *cosmetic* money sink with mechanical edges, never pay-to-win
  ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).

## Online (Solano Online) note

- In online, **cosmetics are the monetization surface** — and only ever cosmetic,
  always also earnable, per the charter ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)). No
  outfit grants power; "drip" is status, not advantage.

## Photo mode synergy

The wardrobe feeds photo mode ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md),
[doc 21](21-the-phone-and-meta-ui.md)) — players style the leads, shoot them
against the city, and share, driving organic marketing.

## Customization design rules

- **Personalize within identity** — the three leads stay recognizably themselves.
- **Every cosmetic layer can have a mechanical shadow** (concealment, weather,
  faction read) — but mechanics never *force* an ugly meta; expression stays free.
- **Inclusive options** — broad body, skin-tone, hair-texture, and cultural-dress
  representation, made with care, never as caricature ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).
