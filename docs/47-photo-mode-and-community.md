# 47 — Photo Mode & Community

> Port Soleil is **built to be photographed** ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)) — a
> golden-hour, neon, water-and-storm city. Photo mode and the community/creator
> layer turn that beauty into the game's **best, cheapest marketing** and give
> players reasons to keep living in the city. This doc specs both.

## Photo mode (a launch, first-class feature)

### Why it's a priority, not a bonus
- The art team's work ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)) deserves a showcase, and
  player screenshots are **organic reach** no ad budget buys.
- It deepens players' relationship with the city and the three leads
  ([doc 03](03-characters.md)) — you photograph what you love.

### Feature set
- **Free camera** with full control (orbit, dolly, roll, FOV), detached or
  character-locked.
- **Time & weather scrub** where permitted (golden hour on demand; summon the
  storm light, [doc 38](38-dynamic-events-calendar.md)).
- **Filters & film stocks** that fit the world (period stocks, neon push, faded
  warmth) — original looks, not real-brand presets
  ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).
- **Depth of field, exposure, color grade, vignette, grain** — real photographic
  controls.
- **Posing & expression** for the leads; **costume** via the wardrobe
  ([doc 29](29-wardrobe-and-customization.md)); hide HUD/other players.
- **Action capture** — pause mid-chase, mid-stunt, mid-Redline
  ([doc 42](42-signature-abilities.md)) and frame the moment.
- **Logos/watermark optional**, EXIF-light, easy share.

### In-fiction integration
- Lives in the **phone's Camera app** ([doc 21](21-the-phone-and-meta-ui.md)) — a
  diegetic gesture, not a sterile menu.
- Feeds **collectible photo-hunt** content (snap specific landmarks/murals,
  [doc 18](18-side-stories.md)) and the satirical **Plumage** feed
  ([doc 14](14-brand-bible.md)) — post your crimes, in-fiction.

## Sharing & galleries

- **In-game gallery** (browse, favorite, re-edit) and **easy export/share** to
  platform and social.
- **Curated community galleries** (in-client featured shots) — celebrate players,
  drive aspiration, surface the art.
- **Photo challenges / seasonal themes** tied to the live calendar
  ([doc 30](30-post-launch-roadmap.md)) — "golden hour on the causeway,"
  "the eye of the storm" — light, recurring engagement.

## Community features (beyond photos)

- **Clips & highlights** — easy capture/share of emergent moments (the genre's
  retellable-story engine, [doc 01](01-vision.md)); a "best of my session" reel.
- **Crew identity & galleries** (online, [doc 45](45-solano-online-deepdive.md)) —
  show off your crew, your fleet, your clubhouse.
- **Leaderboards** for the *skill* content (races, time trials, stunt jumps) —
  never for grind or spend ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).

## Creator tools (post-launch horizon)

Explored after launch, with moderation ([doc 30](30-post-launch-roadmap.md)):

- **Custom races & jobs** — players author point-to-points, checkpoint circuits,
  and simple objective jobs in the existing world.
- **Director/replay mode** — a deeper machinima/cinematics toolset building on
  photo mode (camera paths, timing) for community filmmakers.
- **Sharing & discovery hub** — rate, feature, and curate community creations,
  with strong reporting/moderation ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).

## Moderation & safety (non-negotiable)

- **Robust reporting, filtering, and human + automated moderation** on all shared
  content (photos, clips, crew names, creations) ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).
- **Clear community guidelines**; fast action on hate/harassment; the same
  punch-up-not-down standard the game itself holds ([doc 44](44-themes-and-meaning.md)).
- **Privacy-respecting** sharing (opt-in, controllable).

## Accessibility of capture/share

- Photo mode and sharing are **fully navigable** with accessibility settings
  ([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)) — assistive controls, readable UI, no
  twitch-only requirements.

## Design rules

- **Photograph what we're proud of** — if a vista, a lead, or a moment isn't worth
  a screenshot, the art/feel work isn't done ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md),
  [doc 39](39-game-feel-and-juice.md)).
- **Community is marketing** — make it trivially easy to capture and share the
  game's beauty and the player's best stories ([doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)).
- **Safety first** — every shared surface is moderated and reportable
  ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).
- **Original looks only** — filters, presets, and tools carry our identity, not a
  competitor's ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).
