# 52 — The Player Journey (Minute One to Credits)

> Most docs describe *systems*; this one describes the **felt experience** — the
> emotional and practical arc of an actual player from boot-up to the ending. It's
> the sanity check that all the systems add up to a *journey*, not a pile of
> features ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).

## Minute 0–5 — "Is this for me?"
Boot, a clean first-run setup (difficulty axes + accessibility framed kindly,
[doc 23](23-accessibility.md)), then the **cold open**: the gorgeous drone-glide
over Port Soleil, the storm warning, the cut to a sweaty motel
([doc 16](16-vertical-slice-prologue.md)). **Feeling:** *this place is beautiful and
something's wrong.* The hook is set before a button is pressed.

## Minute 5–40 — The prologue — "I get it now"
The crew comes together through the botched score; the player learns driving,
stealth, combat, and — climactically — the **switch**, as survival
([doc 16](16-vertical-slice-prologue.md)). **Feeling:** competence and chemistry —
*these three are fun, and switching between them is the coolest thing I've done in
a game like this in a while.* Ends on the welding hook: hunted, together.

## Hour 1–3 — First freedom — "I want to live here"
The city opens. A first free morning in the Cut, a hustle, the first shops and the
phone ([doc 33](33-district-deepdive-the-cut.md),
[doc 21](21-the-phone-and-meta-ui.md)). The **five-minute corner** quietly does its
work ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)). **Feeling:** *I could just drive around and
be happy* — the open-world hook lands. First retellable emergent moment likely
happens here.

## Act I — "Small Fish" — "I'm building something"
First player-planned heist (Wash Day, [doc 22](22-sample-mission-script.md)), the
first front and laundering, first allies (Mama Yolette), the discovery of the
Vega–Crane war ([doc 04](04-story.md)). **Feeling:** going from desperate to
*operating* — the empire and the crew start to feel like *mine*.

## Act II — "Crossfire" — "I'm in over my head (in a good way)"
The double game; growing scores, crew, and capability; the leads' personal stories
deepen ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md), [doc 36](36-character-bios.md)). The storm
clock starts ticking on the radio ([doc 37](37-radio-programming.md)). The
**midpoint betrayal** lands ([doc 48](48-narrative-branching-system.md)).
**Feeling:** mastery *and* dread — *I'm powerful now, but I've made enemies, and
something's coming.*

## Act II climax — The Drawbridge Job — "everything I've learned, at once"
The marquee set-piece where the double game collapses
([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)). **Feeling:** a peak — *that was the best mission
yet* — followed by the gut-drop of the betrayal/turn. The game pivots from "playing
sides" to "playing for ourselves."

## Act III — "Landfall" — "the city is dying and I have to choose"
Hurricane Odette transforms the world: thinned law, flooding, blackouts, the storm
mega-event ([doc 38](38-dynamic-events-calendar.md)). The penultimate storm score,
then **the Ark Gala** ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)). **Feeling:** operatic stakes
and real weight — *this is bigger than the money now.* Everything the systems have
been arguing ([doc 44](44-themes-and-meaning.md)) comes due.

## The ending — "what was my piece ever for?"
The branching finale ([doc 48](48-narrative-branching-system.md)) offers the three
endings the player *earned* — Clean Break, Scorched Earth, or High Ground — plus
each lead's **personal coda** ([doc 04](04-story.md)). **Feeling:** the thesis lands
as a *choice the player makes*, not a cutscene they watch — *did I run, burn it
down, or stay and fight for home?* The most thematically resonant beat in the game.

## After credits — "I'm not done with this city"
New Game+, the open world to keep living in, side arcs to finish, the empire to
complete, photo mode to play with ([doc 43](43-achievements-and-completion.md),
[doc 47](47-photo-mode-and-community.md)), and — for those who want it — Solano
Online ([doc 45](45-solano-online-deepdive.md)). **Feeling:** *I want more time in
Port Soleil* — and now there's a place to get it.

## The emotional throughline (what we're managing)

```
intrigue → competence → belonging → ambition → dread → mastery
        → reckoning → CHOICE → catharsis → "I want more"
```

If a stretch of the game doesn't move the player *somewhere* on this line, pacing
isn't done ([doc 35](35-mission-flow-and-pacing.md)).

## The session-shape promise (recap, [doc 01](01-vision.md))

Any ~2-hour session anywhere in this journey should deliver: a story/heist beat, a
register contrast (a laugh *or* a heart-punch), player-led sandbox time, and **at
least one emergent moment worth retelling.** That promise holds from hour 1 to the
credits and beyond.

## The journey test (review gate)

> *Watch a real player, start to finish. Can they, unprompted, narrate their
> journey as a **story** — "first I was just trying to survive, then I was building
> a crew, then everything went wrong, and at the end I chose to…"?* If the systems
> didn't add up to that sentence, we built features, not a game
> ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).
