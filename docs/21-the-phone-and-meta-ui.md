# 21 — The Phone & Meta UI

> The in-fiction smartphone is the player's **hub**: missions, contacts, the map,
> the economy, satire, and the camera all live in one diegetic device. Every app
> is invented ([doc 14](14-brand-bible.md)). Modern, familiar, and mockable.

## Why a phone hub

- **Diegetic > menu.** Pulling out a phone beats pausing to a sterile menu — it
  keeps the player in the world and in character.
- **One home for the meta-game** — the same gesture for jobs, money, contacts, and
  selfies.
- **A satire surface.** Apps, ads, notifications, and group chats carry a huge
  share of the comedy and theme without cutscenes.
- **Per-lead identity.** Each lead's phone (wallpaper, apps, chat tone) reflects
  who they are — switching leads switches phones.

## The home screen (apps)

| App | Function | Tone / theme |
|---|---|---|
| **Contacts** | Call/text people to start jobs, ask favors, trigger help | The relationship web in your pocket |
| **Map / Beacon** | Map, waypoints, fast-travel, district intel, flood/storm overlays | "Safety" app that's really surveillance ([doc 14](14-brand-bible.md)) |
| **Ledger** | Money (dirty/clean/crew), businesses, laundering, bank | The economy dashboard ([doc 10](10-economy-progression.md)) |
| **Plumage** | In-fiction social feed: reacts to your crimes & the city's mood | The satire engine; Storm Chasers live here |
| **Vantgo** | Hail a ride (fast-travel), or *be* a driver (a hustle) | Gig-economy mockery |
| **Hodl'em** | Crypto mini-game / money sink | Volatile by design; lose-your-shirt comedy |
| **Camera / Photo** | First-class photo mode ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)) | Organic marketing + collectible photo hunts |
| **Iris** | Theo's tool persona: hacking, contacts, mission intel | Original in-fiction assistant, not a real-brand AI |
| **Music** | Radio control + player playlists ([doc 13](13-radio-music.md)) | The dial in your hand |
| **Job Board** | Hustles, chop-shop orders, bounties | The repeatable-content firehose |

## Notifications & living feed

- **Reactive pushes:** factions react, a business is under attack, a contact has a
  job, Storm Watch escalates, a heist's heat is cooling. The phone is how the
  living world ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)) talks to the player.
- **Group chats** between the three leads carry low-stakes character banter and
  comedy between missions — cheap, high-value characterization.
- **Satirical ad spam** the player can mute (and the muting is a joke too).

## The map (a system, not a screen)

- **Stylized tourist-brochure aesthetic** that turns ominous with flood and storm
  overlays as the season advances ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)).
- **Player-set waypoints, custom markers, and intel pins** (Theo's tags surface
  here).
- **Honest fog:** the map fills in as you explore; icons are *invitations, not
  chores* (pillar/anti-pillar in [doc 01](01-vision.md)) — toggleable density so
  it never becomes a checklist museum.
- **Flood line is drawn on it** — the thesis as UI.

## HUD philosophy (recap & spec)

- **Minimal, diegetic-leaning, fully customizable** ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)).
- **Contextual:** mini-map, Heat indicator, ammo, and objective appear when
  relevant and fade when not.
- **Heat readout is honest:** shows *why* you're wanted and *where they think you
  are* ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)) — escape is skill, not luck.
- **The Crew Wheel** (radial switch UI) is the one bespoke, signature HUD element.

## Pause menu / meta (the non-phone layer)

For things that don't belong on a phone:
- **Character** (skills, appearance, loadout), **Garage/Vehicles**,
  **Properties**, **Stats & progress**, **Settings/Accessibility**
  ([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)), **Save/Load**, **Mission replay/checkpoints**.

## Design rules for the UI

- **Never break the world for information** when a diegetic surface can carry it.
- **Comedy is content** — the phone earns its screen time with jokes and theme,
  not just utility.
- **Customizable to invisible** — a player who wants a clean frame can turn nearly
  all of it off; a player who wants data can surface it.
- **Original visual language throughout** ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).
