# 12 — Legal Distinctiveness & Originality Firewall

> **Purpose.** This document keeps NEON DELTA a clean, original work. It exists so
> that anyone — designer, writer, or lawyer — can verify we are inspired by a
> *genre*, never copying a specific *product*. **This is design guidance, not
> legal advice;** real counsel should review before any commercial release.

## The core principle

**Genres and mechanics are not protected. Specific expression is.**

You **cannot** copyright or trademark:
- The idea of an **open world**, **driving**, **shooting**, or a **crime sandbox**.
- Genre mechanics: wanted levels, heists, switchable characters, car theft,
  radio stations, mini-maps.
- Real-world inspiration: warm-weather American cities, cartels, corrupt cops.

You **can** protect (and we must therefore avoid copying): specific **names,
characters, logos, brands, story, dialogue, art, music, and trade dress** of any
existing product.

## Our firewall rules

1. **Original geography.** Port Soleil, Solano County, the Sablon Coast, and the
   state of Verano are invented. We do **not** name, map, or replicate any real
   or trademarked fictional city. No "[Real City] with the serial numbers filed
   off" — the layout, landmarks, and identity are our own.
2. **Original people.** Every character name, likeness, backstory, and voice is
   created here. No analog is a renamed version of an existing character.
3. **Original brands.** Every in-world brand, radio station, vehicle make, weapon,
   and product is invented (see the Brand Bible). Vehicles are **inspired-by
   silhouettes**, never badge-accurate clones of real trademarked models or
   logos.
4. **Original story & dialogue.** Plot, missions, and script are wholly authored.
   No scene, line, or set-piece is lifted from an existing work.
5. **Original UI/trade dress.** HUD, menus, typography, logo, map style, and
   marketing language are our own visual identity — not a pastiche of a
   competitor's recognizable look.
6. **Original audio.** Music is licensed or original composition; no soundalikes
   commissioned to imitate a specific identifiable track or artist.
7. **Satire, not appropriation.** We satirize *types* of institutions
   (developers, megachurches, gig apps), not identifiable real companies or
   people in a defamatory way.

## Naming-collision checklist (per asset)

Before any name ships, verify it is **not**:
- A registered trademark in games/entertainment (search before locking).
- The name of a well-known character, place, or product in the genre.
- A trivial misspelling/homophone of a protected mark (no "filing the serial
  numbers off").
- A real person's name used in a way implying endorsement or defaming them.

> Track cleared names in a future `docs/12a-cleared-names-registry.md`.

## Vehicles & "inspired-by" boundaries

- Real car *silhouettes* and *archetypes* (a muscle car, a supercar) are fair
  inspiration; **logos, model names, and exact trademarked designs are not.**
- We design **original makes and models** with their own badges and proportions,
  the way the genre standardly does. Distinctive enough to avoid confusion.

## Music & radio

- Radio stations, DJ personas, and station branding are invented.
- Tracks are **either originally composed or properly licensed**; we never
  commission "make it sound exactly like [specific song]."

## What we *deliberately keep* (and why it's fine)

| We keep | Why it's safe |
|---|---|
| Open-world structure | Genre convention, unprotectable |
| Driving / shooting / heists | Mechanics, unprotectable |
| Wanted/Heat system | Mechanic; ours has original rules & UI |
| Switchable protagonists | Mechanic; our characters are original |
| Crime-satire tone | Genre/voice; our targets & jokes are original |
| Radio stations | Format is generic; our stations & music are original |

## Process gates

- **Originality review** at concept lock for every named asset.
- **Legal review** before any vertical slice, marketing, or release.
- **Documentation:** keep this firewall current; log cleared names; flag any
  asset that drifts toward a recognizable real product and redesign it.

## One-sentence summary

**We are building an original game in a shared genre — taking the verbs everyone
is free to use and pairing them with a world, a cast, and a voice that are
entirely our own.**
