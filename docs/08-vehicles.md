# 08 — Vehicles & Driving

## Philosophy

Driving is the **soul of traversal** and Rae's home turf. The model aims for
**arcade-sim**: accessible and joyful on a controller, but with real weight,
grip, and personality per vehicle. Every car should feel like a character.

## The driving model

- **Weight & grip simulation** tuned for *fun first*: believable mass transfer,
  controllable slides, and a satisfying handbrake. Not a hardcore sim, not a
  weightless go-kart.
- **Damage that matters:** cosmetic, performance (engine, tires, handling), and
  catastrophic. Smoke, blowouts, and a dying engine create tension in a chase.
- **Surface & weather feedback:** wet roads, flooded streets, sand, mud, and
  swamp all change handling. The tide literally changes the road.
- **Redline (Rae's signature):** a focus state slowing time for perfect lines,
  near-misses, and stunts — turns a chase into choreography.

## Vehicle classes

| Class | Examples (invented brands) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Compacts & econoboxes** | *Kessler Pip*, *Solé Mite* | Stealth, blending in |
| **Sedans & coupes** | *Verano Marlin*, *Crest Deluxe* | Daily drivers, getaway base |
| **Muscle** | *Brand-X Maraschino*, *Halcyon Comet* | Tire-smoking American iron |

| **Aircraft** | *Pelican seaplane*, helicopters, ultralights | Late-game freedom |

## Boats & the water (a co-equal traversal layer)

Because half the county is water, **boats are not an afterthought** — they're a
parallel road system. Smuggling, escapes, and whole missions live at sea or in
the canals. **Airboats** rule the Glades; the **tide** opens and closes channels;
storms turn the sea into a hazard and a hiding place.

## Aircraft

Earned, not given — late-game traversal and heist tools. Seaplanes and
helicopters for the Keys and the swamp; ultralights for stunt-smuggling. Flight
is forgiving-arcade, with weather as a real risk near a storm.

## Customization & ownership

- **Garages & ownership:** owned vehicles persist, can be tracked, stolen, and
  recovered; insurance/recovery system so loss stings without being punishing.
- **Visual mods:** paint (including weathered/salt-worn finishes that fit the
  city), wraps, rims, body kits, plates, interiors.
- **Performance mods:** engine, transmission, brakes, suspension, tires, nitrous,
  armor — tuned to class and to legality (some mods raise Heat).
- **Rae's specialty:** unique handling tunes and stunt parts only she can fit.

## Acquisition

- **Steal it** (and risk the owner reporting it / the Heat).
- **Buy it** (legit dealers, gray-market, or auctions).
- **Win it** (races, pink slips).
- **Earn it** (story rewards, hidden/abandoned vehicles, storm-stranded finds).

## Theft & the world's reaction

Grand vehicle theft is a core verb — and the world responds:
- **Hotwiring** is a quick skill-check minigame; nicer cars are harder.
- **Owners react:** some call it in (Heat), some chase, some are armed.
- **Hot vehicles** are flagged: keep a stolen car too long near cops and it's a
  liability. Re-plating/respraying at a shop cools it down.

## Signature vehicle moments (design targets)

- A causeway chase at king tide with waves breaking over the deck.
- An airboat pursuit through the Glades as the tide drains the channels.
- A supercar run down the Strip at night, neon smearing in *Redline*.
- A seaplane escape lifting off a flooded street as the hurricane lands.
