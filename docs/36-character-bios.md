## Rae Castellano — *The Wheel*

**Born** in the Cut, above the garage, to Tomás and a mother who left for the
touring circuit and never really came back. Rae learned cars before she learned to
read — Tomás's hands guiding hers on a wrench. She was the fastest kid on the
causeway and got out the only honest way the Cut offers: **talent.** Hollywood
precision-driving, fifteen years, the best in the business, a name in the credits
nobody reads.

**The wound:** a stunt went wrong on a picture she begged off of and got pulled
back onto. A younger driver — her protégé — died in the car Rae was supposed to be
in. The studios closed ranks; the lawyers buried it; the calls stopped. She came
home to a sinking neighborhood, a father whose hands were starting to shake, and a
guilt she drives to outrun.

**What she wants (says):** out, clean, with enough to fix the garage and take care
of Tomás. **What she wants (truth):** to forgive herself — to be *in the car* when
it matters and not lose anyone this time. That's why she can't leave the crew: they
*are* the car, and she's not running from this one.

**Voice:** low, dry, economical. Says less than she knows. The conscience that
keeps the ledger. Catchphrase: *"That's Soleil"* ([doc 25](25-glossary-slang.md)).

**Arc question:** does saving people mean saving the garage (the past) or letting
it go to save the *people* (the future)? Her coda ([doc 04](04-story.md)) turns on
it.

## Theo "Ghost" Okafor — *The Key*

**Born** to Nigerian immigrant parents in Sabal Springs; raised on his mother's
discipline and his father's quiet faith in systems — *if you understand how a
thing works, you can fix it.* A scholarship, a degree, and a recruiter who told him
he'd be "protecting people." For three years he built surveillance for a private
intelligence firm and told himself the data was keeping someone safe.

**The wound:** he found out what the data did — who got disappeared, who got
ruined, with tools *he* built. He pulled the files to expose it and ran when he
realized he was the only witness who'd be believed dead. The firm blacklisted him,
froze his life, and made it clear his sister **Ada** was leverage. He's been a
ghost ever since, brilliant and cornered, his greatest skill turned into the thing
hunting him.

**What he wants (says):** stay invisible, get Ada clear, disappear for real. **What
he wants (truth):** to *matter* loudly once — to use the weapon they made of him
against the people who made it, even if it costs him the safety he's built his
whole self around.

**Voice:** quiet, precise, deadpan-funny — literal where others are dramatic.
Notices everything, says little, means all of it. Dry corrections of Frankie are a
running bit ([doc 22](22-sample-mission-script.md)).

**Arc question:** can he be free without being seen? His Ghost-in-the-Machine arc
([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)) forces the choice between hiding and *standing.*

## Frankie "Sticks" Delgado — *The Mouth*

**Born** in the Glades and the Cut both — Mama Yolette's network practically raised
him after his folks washed out. Charming since before he could walk, Frankie has
spent his life one scheme from the big win and one payment behind. ("Sticks"
because he always says he'll *stick* this time, and never does — the nickname is a
loving insult.) He'd give you his last dollar and then borrow two of yours.

**The wound:** Frankie has never once been *enough* — not for his folks, not for
**Lucia** (the one who got away, now deep in the Gospel of Gain), not for himself.
Every con is an audition for a worth he can't feel. The botched score that opens
the game is *his* "sure thing" — the time it was finally going to be different —
and it nearly gets his only real family killed.

**What he wants (says):** the score, the win, to finally be *the guy.* **What he
wants (truth):** to be worth keeping — and to learn the crew already kept him,
debts and all, before any of the money.

**Voice:** fast, bright, never stops, all heart and bad math. The comedy engine and
the soul of the group. Catchphrases: *"It's a sure thing," "Define clean"*
([doc 25](25-glossary-slang.md)).

**Arc question:** does he finally *win* — or learn that walking away with the
people who love him *is* the win? His coda offers both, and a darker third
([doc 04](04-story.md)).

## Lourdes "La Señora" Vega — *the established order*

Daughter of dockworkers who clawed a smuggling foothold into a thirty-year
institution. Lourdes took the family **legitimate-ish** and ruthless, and she truly
believes she is the levee holding back chaos — that without the Vegas, Port
Soleil's old families would have been devoured decades ago. **She is not wrong, and
that's what makes her dangerous.** The crew's score didn't just cost her money; it
proved, publicly, that the levee leaks. Tragic, principled, lethal.

---

## Sterling Crane — *the new order*

Out-of-state money with no roots and no nostalgia. Crane looked at a coast everyone
was trying to *save* and saw the smartest trade of his life: **don't fight the
flood — own what's left when it wins.** Charming, modern, philanthropic on camera,
utterly without a floor. He buys the high ground, the cops, the weather data, and
the relief funds, and calls it stewardship. **The flood is his business plan.** The
apex predator the Vegas are too old to recognize until it's late.

---

## Det. Marcus "Hound" Boyd — *the law that isn't*

A real talent gone rancid: Boyd closes cases, and he stopped caring years ago
whether the person who goes down did it. He rents his badge to whoever's paying and
sleeps fine. The crew made him look *stupid* — caught on his own scene — and that's
the one thing his ego can't expense. He hunts them less for justice than for the
photograph that embarrassed him. The human face of an institution that's just
another gang with a pension.

## Bio guardrails

- **Wounds drive plot:** each lead's specific hurt is what the antagonists press —
  no backstory is decoration ([doc 04](04-story.md)).
- **Antagonists are people, not evil:** each believes he/she is the reasonable one;
  the tragedy is that they're partly right ([doc 03a](03a-cast-relationships.md)).
- **Specificity over stereotype:** detailed, individual, melting-pot lives written
  with care — never a demographic shorthand ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).
