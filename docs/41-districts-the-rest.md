# 41 — The Rest of the Map (District Briefs)

> The Cut got the full ship-quality treatment ([doc 33](33-district-deepdive-the-cut.md))
> as the production template. This doc brings the **other six districts** up to a
> consistent design level — identity, landmarks, system showcase, and signature
> content — so the whole county is specced ([doc 06](06-map-districts.md)). Each
> reuses the Cut's pipeline and must clear the same quality bar
> ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).

---

## Downtown Core — *"The Strip"*

**Identity:** vertical neon spectacle; the city's id and its money. Towers, deco
hotels, casinos, clubs, the financial district, Crane's flagship tower.
- **Landmarks:** the Crane Tower (navigation anchor & H-target), the Lucky Manatee
  casino (Vega), the Velvet Room club, the financial canyon.
- **System showcase:** **verticality** (rooftop/helipad heists), max **crowd
  density** for vanish-into-the-crowd Heat play, highest security/police response,
  night-cycle nightlife ([doc 38](38-dynamic-events-calendar.md)).
- **Tide/storm role:** low avenues flood at surge; towers stay lit on generators —
  the high/low divide *within one district* ([doc 02](02-setting-world.md)).
- **Signature content:** casino-adjacent scores, rooftop chases, club hustles, the
  financial-district set-piece; supercar valet temptations ([doc 27](27-vehicle-catalog.md)).

---

## Marisol Heights — *"The Dry Side"*

**Identity:** the only real high ground; old-money estates, gated communities,
country clubs, private security. Where the people buying the city live.
- **Landmarks:** Highline Estates gates, the country club, Crane's private
  compound, the reservoir on the hill.
- **System showcase:** **infiltration & stealth** (the marquee — see H6 Highline,
  [doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)), faction = private security not street crews,
  hostile to outsiders by design ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)).
- **Tide/storm role:** **stays dry** — the entire point of who lives here; the
  thesis rendered as geography ([doc 24](24-world-lore-timeline.md)).
- **Signature content:** the gala/estate infiltrations, the rolling-blackout heist,
  recon-heavy jobs; the quiet menace of wealth ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)).

---

## The Reach (Hialto Industrial)

**Identity:** the working port — container yards, rail, refineries, warehouses; the
Vega family's logistical heart. Gritty, mechanical, full of cover.
- **Landmarks:** the container cranes, the rail yard, the refinery flare stacks,
  Vega's dock offices.
- **System showcase:** **vehicle & crane set-pieces**, industrial gunfights with
  layered cover, smuggling logistics, heavy-vehicle heist tools
  ([doc 27](27-vehicle-catalog.md)); H4 Repossession lives here
  ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)).
- **Tide/storm role:** toxic flood risk; dramatic storm chaos at the docks;
  blackout-prone ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)).
- **Signature content:** container heists, the crane sequence, port-smuggling,
  faction-war flashpoints (Vega home turf).

---

## Cayo Brava & the Keys

**Identity:** barrier-island paradise across a long causeway; beaches, marinas,
party houses, hidden coves, Coast Guard blind spots.
- **Landmarks:** the lighthouse (navigation anchor), the marina, the party-house
  strip, the smuggling coves.
- **System showcase:** **boats & the water network** at full expression
  ([doc 08](08-vehicles.md)), open beach-road driving theater, sea races, the
  causeway chase arena ([doc 06](06-map-districts.md)); H3 Open Water
  ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)).
- **Tide/storm role:** **first to evacuate** — eerie and empty as the storm nears;
  the most beautiful and the most exposed ([doc 38](38-dynamic-events-calendar.md)).
- **Signature content:** smuggling runs, the regatta event, beach hustles, hidden
  diveable caches at low tide ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)).

---

## Bayou Verde / The Glades

**Identity:** the wild swamp interior; airboats, fishing camps, hideouts, an
alligator-farm front, off-grid militias, Mama Yolette's network. Lawless, beautiful,
threatening.
- **Landmarks:** Mama Yo's supper club, the gator farm, the militia camps, the
  hidden hideouts deep in the channels.
- **System showcase:** the **most dynamic tide terrain** (channels open/close with
  the tide), **airboat chases** ([doc 08](08-vehicles.md)), ambush-heavy combat,
  off-grid hideout fantasy.
- **Tide/storm role:** the tide constantly reshapes navigable water — the living
  environment at its most alive ([doc 02](02-setting-world.md)).
- **Signature content:** the Open Water arc base ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)),
  airboat smuggling, militia encounters, "going to the Glades" to lie low
  ([doc 25](25-glossary-slang.md)).

---

## Sabal Springs

**Identity:** the faded inland suburb — strip malls, motels, megachurches, a dead
mall, trailer parks; the working poor and the "real America" the postcards skip.
- **Landmarks:** the Gospel of Gain megachurch, the dead mall, the highway
  motels, the trailer parks.
- **System showcase:** **horizontal high-speed driving** (sprawling roads), the
  comedy-satire register (megachurch, strip-mall America), suburban-score stealth;
  H5 the Gospel Cut ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)).
- **Tide/storm role:** ironically **safer ground** — where the displaced from the
  flooded coast end up; melancholy ([doc 24](24-world-lore-timeline.md)).
- **Signature content:** the Gospel of Gain arc ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)),
  Frankie's roots, dead-mall encounters, the Storm Chasers' satire of catastrophe.

## Consistency rules across the map

- **Every district passes the Cut's quality gates** ([doc 33](33-district-deepdive-the-cut.md)):
  the five-minute-corner test, multi-time/tide moods, no filler blocks, unmistakable
  art identity.
- **Each owns a distinct system fantasy** (vertical heists / stealth / industrial /
  water / swamp / horizontal-satire) so traversal *between* them feels like
  crossing worlds ([doc 06](06-map-districts.md)).
- **The flood line runs through all of them** — every district has a clear
  high/low-ground story; that's the game's thesis as level geometry
  ([doc 01](01-vision.md), [doc 24](24-world-lore-timeline.md)).
