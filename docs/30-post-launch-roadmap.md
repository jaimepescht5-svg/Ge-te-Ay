# 30 — Post-Launch & Seasonal Roadmap

> How Port Soleil keeps living after launch — for both the single-player world and
> Solano Online ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)) — without betraying the
> pillars ([doc 01](01-vision.md)) or the monetization charter. **Content, not
> paywalls. The world evolves; it doesn't reset.**

## Principles

1. **The base game is complete.** Post-launch *adds*; it never withholds the
   finished campaign to sell back in pieces.
2. **The world has continuity.** The flood keeps rising, factions keep warring,
   the city keeps changing — seasons are chapters in one ongoing story, not
   disconnected battle-pass themes.
3. **Earnable-first, always.** Anything buyable is also playable-for; no
   pay-to-win, no FOMO treadmill, no gambling-for-power
   ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).
4. **Fix before flex.** Stability, performance, and accessibility patches lead;
   shiny content follows. No content cadence that requires crunch
   ([doc 20](20-tech-and-production.md)).

## Phase 0 — Launch window (first 90 days)

- **Stability, performance, balance** patches as the priority.
- **Accessibility follow-through** — act on real-player feedback fast
  ([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)).
- **Photo-mode & community features** polish (the organic-marketing engine,
  [doc 15](15-art-direction.md)).
- **Solano Online soft expansion** — quality-of-life, anti-cheat, moderation
  hardening before any big content drops.

## Single-player story expansions (premium, substantial)

Two planned narrative expansions — **full arcs, new content, fair price**:

- **Expansion 1 — "Undertow."** A prequel-adjacent arc: the **Vega century**
  ([doc 24](24-world-lore-timeline.md)) — play a younger La Señora's rise, or the
  crew's earliest jobs. New southern-Keys map area, new heists, new mechanics.
- **Expansion 2 — "After the Water."** A future-set epilogue arc: Port Soleil
  *after* a major flood — a changed map (drowned districts, new high-ground
  shantytowns), exploring the consequences of the ending the player chose.

> Expansions add content and systems; they **never** wall off the base game's
> existing features.

## Seasonal live content (Solano Online — free core)

Seasons themed to the city's living calendar — each ~free content drop plus
optional cosmetic store ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)):

| Season theme | Hook | Sample content |
|---|---|---|
| **Storm Season** | The hurricane returns | Co-op storm-rescue & looting events, weather-only heists |
| **Festival of Lights** | A city street festival | Parade-heist, race series, music/radio event ([doc 13](13-radio-music.md)) |
| **Election Year** | The city votes (satire) | Faction-war flashpoints, dirt-digging missions, the Reclamation escalates |
| **High Tide** | King-tide flooding peaks | Boat-focused content, sunken-cache dives, the water network expands |
| **Boom Town** | A development gold rush | Business wars, new fronts, Crane Coastal escalation |

- **Living-world continuity:** each season *moves the city forward* — a district
  floods, a faction gains ground, a new business opens — so returning players see
  a changed Port Soleil.
- **Earnable rewards** for playing the season; **cosmetics-only** store; no
  power for sale.

## Free updates (all players)

- **New vehicles, weapons, wardrobe** ([docs 27](27-vehicle-catalog.md),
  [28](28-weapon-catalog.md), [29](29-wardrobe-and-customization.md)) seeded into
  the world to find/earn.
- **New hustles, Strangers, and world events** ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)).
- **Quality-of-life & accessibility** improvements as ongoing commitments.
- **New game+ / replay** options for the campaign (approach the heists fresh).

## Community & creation (longer horizon)

- **Photo-mode galleries & sharing** as a launch feature; **creator tools** /
  custom races & jobs explored post-launch (with moderation).
- **In-fiction ARG-light seasonal storytelling** (Storm Watch broadcasts, Plumage
  feeds) that bleeds the world's continuity into the meta.

## What we will NOT do (hard lines)

- ❌ Sell power, randomized loot boxes, or gambling-for-advantage.
- ❌ Inflate grind to pressure purchases.
- ❌ Gate base-game systems behind paid passes.
- ❌ Ship season cadence that demands crunch.
- ❌ Reset the world's continuity for a marketing theme.

## The post-launch promise (one line)

> **Port Soleil keeps living — it floods, it votes, it parties, it changes — and
> everything that matters is something you can play your way to, not pay your way
> into.**
