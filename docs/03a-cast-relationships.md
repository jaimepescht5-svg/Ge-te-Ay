## Hearts (what each lead can lose)

The "heart" is the personal stake the antagonists can threaten — the engine of
each lead's arc and personal coda ([doc 04](04-story.md)).

| Lead | Heart | Threatened by | Coda question |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rae** | Tomás & the garage (the Cut) | Crane's Reclamation | Does she save her home or get out clean? |
| **Theo** | Ada's safety | The intel firm / blacklist | Can he get her free without going loud? |
| **Frankie** | The crew itself (& Lucia) | His own debts / the Vegas | Does he finally win — or die a hero? |

## Antagonist motivations (so they're people, not obstacles)

- **La Señora (Lourdes Vega):** believes she's the *order* holding back chaos; the
  crew's score humiliated her and threatens a thirty-year empire she sees as
  protecting the city's old families. Tragic, not evil.
- **Sterling Crane:** sees the flood as a **liquidation event** — a chance to own a
  drowned coast once the current owners are gone. Charming, modern, utterly
  amoral. The flood is his *business plan*. The real apex predator.
- **Det. Boyd:** a man who confuses *clearing cases* with *justice*, financing a
  comfortable life by renting his badge. The crew made him look stupid once; now
  it's personal. The face of an institution that's just a gang with a budget.

## Allies (and their price)

- **Tomás** — the moral center and fixer; his disapproval is the crew's
  conscience; his health is a clock.
- **Mama Yolette** — opens the Glades and the sea; loyal but protective of *her*
  people first; will not let the crew endanger them.
- **Donnie Pelham** — the inside man; useful and **unstable** — a designed
  flip-risk whose loyalty tracks how the player treats him.
- **Ada** — patches the crew up and **hates that she does**; a recurring moral
  mirror for Theo and the player.
- **Roady (Storm Chasers)** — comic, exploitative, occasionally useful; satire of
  catastrophe-as-content.

## Dynamic relationship values (system)

Key relationships carry a **standing value** the player nudges through choices:

- **Tracked for:** Tomás, Ada, Lucia, Mama Yolette, Pelham, and inter-crew trust.
- **Effects:** unlock/lock missions, change dialogue, shift help offered, and feed
  the **personal codas** and the ending fork.
- **Inter-crew trust** is its own meter — push one lead's agenda too hard at the
  others' expense and friction surfaces in banter, then in mechanics (reluctant
  help, worse standing orders) until repaired.

## Casting & voice direction (intent)

- **Authenticity first:** cast actors whose backgrounds fit the melting-pot
  fiction; multilingual delivery where natural (Spanish/Creole code-switching in
  the Cut and the Glades).
- **Performance-capture** for the leads to carry the heart the satire needs.
- **Voice signatures:** Rae (low, dry, controlled), Theo (quiet, precise, deadpan),
  Frankie (fast, bright, never shuts up) — readable by ear alone.
- **Original characters only** — no voice/likeness derived from any existing
  property ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).
