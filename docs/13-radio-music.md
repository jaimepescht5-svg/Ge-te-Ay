# 13 — Radio & Music

## Why radio matters

In an open-world crime game the **car is a second home**, and the radio is its
heartbeat. Music sets the tone of the whole city, scores a thousand unscripted
moments, and gives Port Soleil its accent. Every station, DJ, and bumper is
**invented** (see [doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)); music is original
composition or properly licensed — never a soundalike.

## The dial

A diegetic radio with a dozen stations, each a different facet of the city.

| Station | Genre | Vibe / role |
|---|---|---|
| **Sablon FM 101.3** | Latin, salsa, cumbia, reggaeton | The soul of Old Soleil; warm, danceable, the city's default heartbeat |
| **Static 99.9** | Storm-warped garage rock & punk | Loud, defiant, the sound of the people getting bulldozed |
| **Brackish Beats** | House, bass, electronic | Nightlife on the Strip; builds with the night cycle |
| **Velvet Hour 88.1** | Soul, funk, slow jams | Golden-hour cruising; Rae's station |
| **The Low End** | Hip-hop, trap, regional | Sabal Springs and the streets; Frankie's station |
| **Coastline Country** | Country, swamp-blues, zydeco | The Glades and the interior; Mama Yolette's world |
| **Frequency 7** | Synthwave, downtempo, ambient electronic | Theo's station; cold, precise, late-night drives |
| **Aurora Classical** | Classical & film-score moods | High society, the Heights, the gala |
| **The Gospel of Gain (AM)** | Talk/satire — prosperity preacher | Satirical talk; world-building & comedy |
| **Storm Watch 24 (AM)** | Talk — disaster news cycle | Dynamic: escalates as the hurricane nears |
| **Pirate signal: Tide Pirate** | Eclectic, illegal, roaming | A roving unlicensed DJ; appears/vanishes; deep-cut gems |
| **Self / silence** | — | Player playlist support; off |

## Dynamic, reactive radio

The dial is **alive**, not a static loop:

- **Talk stations react to the world:** Storm Watch escalates as a hurricane
  builds; news bulletins reference the player's bigger crimes (anonymized) and
  faction events; the Gospel of Gain riffs on the city's mood.
- **Music shifts by time & place:** Brackish Beats peaks at night; Sablon FM runs
  morning shows; stations near a transmitter come in clear, far ones fade to
  static — diegetic signal strength.
- **Storm-warped audio:** as landfall nears, music stations distort, drop out, and
  bleed into emergency broadcasts — the dial itself dramatizes the climax.

## DJs & personalities

Each music station has an invented host with a voice and bits:

- **"Mami" Celeste** (Sablon FM) — warm matriarch, dedications, neighborhood news.
- **Hex** (Static 99.9) — feral, anti-establishment, hates Crane on air.
- **DJ Mirage** (Brackish Beats) — pure hype, lives at night.
- **The Velvet Fox** (Velvet Hour) — smooth, philosophical, after-midnight energy.
- **Pastor Goldwynn** (Gospel of Gain) — the satirical grift-preacher; recurring
  comic antagonist who literally launders money in the plot.

## Original score (non-radio)

Beyond the dial, an **adaptive orchestral-electronic score** underscores missions
and the open world:

- **Layered, system-driven:** intensity tracks Heat tier, mission phase, and storm
  state — stems add/drop on the fly.
- **Leitmotifs per lead:** Rae (warm strings + engine), Theo (cold synth pulse),
  Frankie (brassy, restless), the crew theme (all three woven together).
- **The city's theme:** a recurring melody — gorgeous and melancholy — that says
  *paradise that knows it's drowning.* Heard at sunsets, vistas, and the endings.
- **The storm motif:** a low, building dread-tone that the whole soundtrack bends
  toward in Act III.

## Music licensing & curation philosophy

- A **broad, eclectic, era-spanning** soundtrack that feels like a real city's
  taste, weighted toward warm-climate port-culture genres.
- **Diversity is the point:** the dial should sound like the melting pot the city
  is, not one demographic's playlist.
- **Discovery:** the Tide Pirate and deep-cut rotations reward players who keep
  flipping the dial — finding "your song" for a night drive is a designed moment.

## Signature audio moments (design targets)

- Sunset on the causeway, top down, Velvet Hour playing — the screenshot the game
  sells itself on.
- The dial dissolving into static and emergency tones as the hurricane lands.
- A getaway scored by whatever was *already playing* — the systemic music making
  an unscripted chase feel authored.
