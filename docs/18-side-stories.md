# 18 — Side Stories & Strangers

> The campaign's connective tissue: character-driven arcs that flesh out Port
> Soleil, deepen the leads, feed the economy, and seed ending variables. Each is
> a **4–6 mission arc** with its own cast, mechanical flavor, and a tangible
> reward (a business, a vehicle, a crew specialist, or an ending lever).

## Design role

- **Show the city's life** beyond the main plot's crime machinery.
- **Develop the leads laterally** — who they are when the heat's off.
- **Reward exploration** — the best are found, not handed out.
- **Tonal range** — side stories carry more of the comedy and the heartbreak than
  the main spine has room for.

---

## "The Reclamation" *(theme spine — Crane's machine)*
Following **Crane Coastal's** block-by-block conquest of the Cut: a tenant-union
organizer, a bought inspector, a holdout grandmother, a demolition "accident."
The slow, *legal* crime under the illegal one.
- **Mechanics:** sabotage, evidence-gathering, protecting people and places.
- **Reward:** dirt that feeds the **High Ground** ending; saves (or loses) the
  Cut depending on choices. **Lead focus:** Rae (it's her neighborhood).

## "Storm Chasers" *(satire — disaster-as-content)*
A crew of disaster-looters and **Roady**, a Plumage streamer documenting the
city's death for clout. Equal parts funny and grim.
- **Mechanics:** time-pressure looting during weather events; chases; a recurring
  "go viral or stay alive" tension.
- **Reward:** a unique fast vehicle + storm-looting hustle. **Lead focus:** Frankie.

## "The Gospel of Gain" *(comedy-heist — and a personal wound)*
Beyond H5: **Pastor Goldwynn's** prosperity empire, and **Lucia**, Frankie's old
flame, in too deep. A redemption-or-ruin arc.
- **Mechanics:** social engineering, undercover, a con that spirals.
- **Reward:** the church laundering line (income) + a Frankie arc resolution lever.

## "Open Water" *(the forgotten coast)*
The Keys smuggling world and the migrants/fishermen the Coast Guard overlooks —
**Mama Yolette's** extended network and a young runner she's protecting.
- **Mechanics:** sea routes, evasion, moral choices about who you carry.
- **Reward:** smuggling empire upgrades + a loyal crew specialist (a pilot).
  **Lead focus:** ensemble; deepens the Glades.

## "Ghost in the Machine" *(Theo's blacklist)*
Theo hunts the people who burned him and the firm still hunting his sister
**Ada**. The most thriller-shaped arc.
- **Mechanics:** pure infiltration, data-heists, counter-surveillance.
- **Reward:** Ada's safety (or not) + Theo's signature gear upgrades + an ending
  lever. **Lead focus:** Theo.

## "The Garage" *(Rae's anchor)*
Saving **Tomás's garage** from Crane and Rae's reckoning with the stunt that
ended her old life — a former colleague resurfaces.
- **Mechanics:** street races, stunt jobs, a vehicle-restoration throughline.
- **Reward:** the HQ's full upgrade tree + Rae's unique vehicle + an ending lever.
  **Lead focus:** Rae.

## "Last Call" *(Strangers anthology)*
A loose set of one-to-two-mission **Stranger** encounters — Port Soleil
vignettes: a retiree's revenge, a missing-person case, a streamer's stunt, a
manatee-mascot gone rogue, a wedding that needs crashing. Tonal palette cleansers
from sweet to absurd to dark.
- **Reward:** money, oddball vehicles, collectible unlocks, and flavor — the
  city's short stories.

## Hustles (repeatable, infinite) — recap with detail
Not stories but the economic baseline ([doc 10](10-economy-progression.md)):
- **Courier / delivery** (legit and not), **repo work**, **collections**,
  **chop-shop steal-to-order**, **smuggling runs**, **bounty pickups**,
  **storm rescues** (rare, well-paid, only during weather events).

## Collectibles & world secrets (exploration rewards)
- **Tide-line shrines** — small memorials at the flood line; lore + a reward when
  all found. **Mural hunt** — Old Soleil street art telling the city's history.
  **Sunken caches** — diveable wrecks revealed at low tide. **Pirate signal
  hunt** — track the Tide Pirate's roving transmitter for rare music + a vehicle.

## Side-content design rules
- **Every arc earns its reward** — no filler quest gives a meaningful unlock.
- **Tone variety is mandatory** — schedule comedy and tragedy so the city feels
  whole.
- **At least one arc per lead** resolves their personal heart ([doc 03](03-characters.md)).
- **Choices echo** into faction rep and the ending fork ([doc 04](04-story.md)).
