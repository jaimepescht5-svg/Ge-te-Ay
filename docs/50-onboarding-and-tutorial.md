# 50 — Onboarding & Tutorial Design

> How NEON DELTA teaches a deep, multi-system sandbox **without** a wall of
> tooltips or a patronizing tutorial island. The prologue
> ([doc 16](16-vertical-slice-prologue.md)) is the spine of onboarding; this doc
> specs the philosophy and the full first-few-hours teaching curve.

## Philosophy: teach by playing

- **Diegetic-first.** The story *is* the tutorial — you learn driving because Rae
  drives, stealth because Theo scouts, combat because Frankie fights
  ([doc 16](16-vertical-slice-prologue.md)). No "press X to learn X" island.
- **One new idea at a time, then combine.** Introduce verbs in isolation, then
  remix them under pressure (the prologue's switch climax).
- **Respect the genre-literate, welcome the newcomer.** Veterans can skip prompts;
  newcomers get optional depth. The teaching is *layered*, not forced.
- **Trust the player.** Show, let them try, let them fail safely, then raise the
  stakes ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).

## The onboarding curve (first ~3 hours)

| Phase | Teaches | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Prologue** ([doc 16](16-vertical-slice-prologue.md)) | The 3 verbs + the switch + tone | Story set-piece, one lead per verb, climactic switch |
| **First free morning** | The phone, map, fast-travel, shops | Low-stakes errands around the Cut ([doc 33](33-district-deepdive-the-cut.md)) |
| **First hustle** | The economy loop, fencing, Heat basics | An optional courier/repo job ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)) |
| **H2 "Wash Day"** ([doc 22](22-sample-mission-script.md)) | The heist system, planning, laundering | The first *player-planned* score, approaches introduced gently |
| **First businesses/upgrades** | Progression, properties, signature trees | Story-gated unlock of the garage hub + first skill points |

By hour three the player has *done* every core system once, in fiction, without a
single tutorial wall.

## Teaching the hard stuff (depth, layered)

The genuinely complex systems get **progressive disclosure**:

- **The switch:** taught as survival in the prologue; *mastery* (signature combos,
  [doc 42](42-signature-abilities.md)) emerges over hours via play, hinted not
  lectured.
- **Heists:** first one is rails-light with strong guidance; later ones strip the
  guidance as the player proves competence ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)).
- **Heat:** the honest readout ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)) *is* the teacher —
  the player learns escape by reading what the UI plainly shows.
- **Economy/laundering:** introduced exactly when first needed (after the first big
  dirty take), not front-loaded ([doc 34](34-economy-balance.md)).

## Optional & on-demand learning

- **Iris / the phone** ([doc 21](21-the-phone-and-meta-ui.md)) offers a searchable,
  **non-intrusive help layer** — a "how do I…?" the player pulls, never a popup
  that interrupts.
- **A practice space** (the garage / a range / an empty lot) lets players rehearse
  driving, shooting, and signature abilities at will — opt-in, never required.
- **Contextual hints are toggleable** and fade as a verb is demonstrably mastered
  (no nagging a competent player).

## Failure as a teacher (safe early stakes)

- **Early failure is soft** — generous checkpoints, low economic penalty, clear
  "here's what got you" feedback ([doc 49](49-difficulty-and-balance.md)).
- **The game telegraphs before it tests** — you always meet a mechanic in a safe
  context before it can cost you ([doc 35](35-mission-flow-and-pacing.md)).
- **Legibility is the curriculum** — because the Heat/AI systems are honest
  ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md), [doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)), the player
  *learns the rules by watching them work fairly.*

## Accessibility of onboarding

- **Adjustable pace** — skippable prompts for veterans; repeatable/extended
  guidance for those who want it ([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)).
- **Multi-modal teaching** — visual demo + text + audio for every taught verb, so
  the lesson lands regardless of which channel a player relies on.
- **First-run setup** surfaces difficulty axes and accessibility *before* the
  player needs them, framed clearly and non-judgmentally
  ([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)).

## Design rules

- **Story teaches systems** — no tutorial island, no tooltip wall
  ([doc 16](16-vertical-slice-prologue.md)).
- **One idea, then combine** — isolate, then remix under pressure.
- **Disclose depth progressively** — complexity arrives as the player is ready
  ([doc 42](42-signature-abilities.md)).
- **Help is pulled, not pushed** — on-demand, never interrupting
  ([doc 21](21-the-phone-and-meta-ui.md)).
- **Telegraph before you test; fail soft early** — onboarding is fair by
  construction ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).
