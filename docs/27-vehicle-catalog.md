## How to read the catalog

- **Top Speed / Accel / Grip / Durability** — relative ★1–5 within the game.
- **Role** — what it's *for* in the sandbox.
- All vehicles support ownership, persistence, theft, and (where noted) mods
  ([doc 08](08-vehicles.md)).

## Catalog design rules

- **Every class has a clear fantasy** — no two models occupy the same niche.
- **Tradeoffs, not a ladder** — the fastest car is the most fragile; the
  toughest is slow. No single "best vehicle."
- **The water & air fleets are first-class,** not token — half the county is wet
  ([doc 06](06-map-districts.md)).
- **Acquisition variety** ([doc 08](08-vehicles.md)): steal, buy, win, earn — each
  model leans toward one.
