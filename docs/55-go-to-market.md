# 55 — Go-To-Market Plan

> Beyond the elevator pitch ([doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)): how NEON DELTA
> reaches players — the campaign arc, the beats, the channels, and the
> **promise-keeping** that the marketing must reflect (no over-promising, no
> bait-and-switch). Marketing is a **contract with the player**
> ([doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)).

## Marketing principles

- **Sell the city and the hook first** — the postcard-then-turn, not a feature
  list ([doc 31](31-announce-trailer-script.md)).
- **Show only what we'll ship** — every trailer is a promise
  ([doc 31](31-announce-trailer-script.md)). No bullshots, no fake gameplay.
- **Lead with our differentiators** — the switch, Quiet/Loud/Clever heists, the
  living/drowning city, and the **trust promises** (no P2W, accessibility)
  ([doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)).
- **Let the world market itself** — photo mode, clips, and community are our
  cheapest, most credible reach ([doc 47](47-photo-mode-and-community.md)).
- **Never name a competitor** — define ourselves by our own pillars
  ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).

## Channels

- **Owned:** trailers, dev diaries (the *responsible-dev* story —
  [doc 20](20-tech-and-production.md)), the in-fiction ARG-light (Storm Watch
  broadcasts, Plumage, [doc 38](38-dynamic-events-calendar.md)).
- **Earned:** press hands-on, creators/streamers (the sandbox *generates*
  content), community photo/clip sharing ([doc 47](47-photo-mode-and-community.md)).
- **Paid:** targeted to genre and narrative-action audiences
  ([doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)); the art sells itself in stills.
- **Community:** wishlists, a clear roadmap, and *responsive* dev comms.

## The "trust beat" (a deliberate marketing moment)

A genre where players are weary of monetization and broken launches gives us an
opening most can't take: **publicly commit, before launch**, to —

- **No pay-to-win, no paid loot boxes, no FOMO** ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).
- **Accessibility as a launch pillar**, documented *before* purchase
  ([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)).
- **No-crunch development** as a value, not a slogan ([doc 20](20-tech-and-production.md)).

Done sincerely and *kept*, this is both ethics and the strongest differentiator we
have ([doc 53](53-open-questions-and-decisions.md), D4).

## Storefront & launch hygiene

- **Honest store page** — the consumer blurb ([doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)),
  real gameplay, clear accessibility info, transparent monetization disclosure
  pre-purchase.
- **Reviews-ready build** — day-1 stability and performance are a *marketing* asset
  ([doc 20](20-tech-and-production.md)); a broken launch undoes every trailer.
- **Clear content/rating guidance** so players self-select well
  ([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)).

## Community management

- **Set expectations, then beat them** — under-promise scope, over-deliver polish.
- **Responsive, human comms** — especially on bugs and roadmap
  ([doc 30](30-post-launch-roadmap.md)).
- **Moderated, safe community spaces** — same punch-up-not-down standard as the
  game ([doc 44](44-themes-and-meaning.md), [doc 47](47-photo-mode-and-community.md)).
- **Celebrate players** — feature community photos/clips; the players *are* the
  campaign ([doc 47](47-photo-mode-and-community.md)).

## Anti-patterns (what we won't do)

- ❌ Bullshots, target-render-as-gameplay, or scripted "gameplay" we can't ship.
- ❌ Over-promising scope to win a news cycle ([doc 53](53-open-questions-and-decisions.md)).
- ❌ Hiding monetization or content concerns until after pre-orders.
- ❌ Naming/knocking competitors instead of standing on our own pillars
  ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).
- ❌ Manufactured FOMO / predatory pre-order tiers.

## The one-line GTM thesis

> **Show a city nobody's seen, make three people they'll love, prove the heists are
> theirs to pull — then earn trust by promising less than everyone else and
> *keeping* it.**
