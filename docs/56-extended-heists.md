# 56 — Extended Heists & Optional Scores

> Beyond the 9 campaign heists ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)), these are
> **optional, discoverable scores** — found via contacts, rep, and exploration —
> that reward players who want more of the heist craft without touching the main
> spine. Same four-phase system, same three-approach rule
> ([doc 07](07-missions.md)). Payouts slot into the curve in
> [doc 34](34-economy-balance.md).

---

## EH1 — "The Manatee Count" *(the casino, Downtown)*
**Target:** the **Lucky Manatee** casino vault on a high-roller weekend (Vega-owned).
- **Quiet:** rig the count-room cameras and walk the skim out with the laundry.
- **Loud:** a floor takeover during the boxing match upstairs — chaos as cover.
- **Clever:** rig the high-roller game (Theo) to bankrupt the house's float, then
  rob the emergency cash transfer.
**Reward:** big clean payout + casino laundering front ([doc 10](10-economy-progression.md)).

## EH2 — "Dry Run" *(the desalination plant, the Reach)*
**Target:** Crane Coastal's experimental **water-tech prototype** (worth a fortune
to rivals).
- **Quiet:** swap the prototype for a decoy; nobody knows for days.
- **Loud:** fight through contractor security; haul it out on a flatbed.
- **Clever:** trigger a (fake) contamination alarm; lift it during the evacuation.
**Reward:** sell to a Vega buyer (cash + Vega rep) **or** leak it (Crane −, a
Reclamation lever, [doc 18](18-side-stories.md)). A choice with consequences
([doc 48](48-narrative-branching-system.md)).

## EH3 — "Sunken" *(the wreck, Cayo Brava)*
**Target:** a **drug-sub** scuttled in a cove at the Keys — a fortune underwater,
contested by smugglers and the Coast Guard.
- **Quiet:** night dive, recover cargo, slip out before either party arrives.
- **Loud:** a three-way sea battle (you vs. smugglers vs. patrol).
- **Clever:** tip the Coast Guard to the smugglers, loot during the bust.
**Reward:** contraband economy boost + the **Reaper** boat upgrade
([doc 27](27-vehicle-catalog.md)). Tide-gated ([doc 02](02-setting-world.md)).

## EH4 — "Founders' Gold" *(the museum, Downtown)*
**Target:** the city museum's **Founders' Day** exhibit — Vega family heirlooms on
loan, a public humiliation of La Señora waiting to happen.
- **Quiet:** after-hours infiltration; defeat a modern security suite (Theo's
  showcase).
- **Loud:** smash-and-grab during the gala opening.
- **Clever:** forge the provenance and *swap* the pieces — the theft isn't
  discovered for weeks.
**Reward:** cash + dirt on the Vega legacy ([doc 24](24-world-lore-timeline.md));
escalates Vega heat.

## EH5 — "The Collection Plate" *(rolling, Sabal Springs)*
**Target:** a **mobile** score — the Gospel of Gain's armored "blessing bus"
touring revival stops across Sabal Springs.
- **Quiet:** board at one stop disguised as crew, off-load at the next.
- **Loud:** a highway intercept (a chase-heist, Rae's showcase).
- **Clever:** pose as a rival "ministry" and con them into handing over the load
  for "safekeeping" (Frankie's showcase).
**Reward:** cash + a comedic Pastor Goldwynn humiliation
([doc 37](37-radio-programming.md)).

## EH6 — "Blackout Bazaar" *(storm-only, the Cut)*
**Target:** a **pop-up gray-market** that only convenes during a storm blackout,
trading everything the city's looting frees up.
- **Quiet:** slip in as a buyer, walk out with the night's marquee item.
- **Loud:** take the whole bazaar — but everyone there is armed and desperate.
- **Clever:** *be* the blackout — extend it (Theo's grid hack) and rob in the dark.
**Reward:** rare gear/vehicle + storm-economy unlock; **only available during
storm events** ([doc 38](38-dynamic-events-calendar.md)).

## EH7 — "The Reservoir Job" *(the Heights)*
**Target:** the **water-rights ledger** in a Highline Estates compound — proof Crane
is hoarding the dry-side water supply.
- **Quiet:** the marquee infiltration of the *driest, most-guarded* ground
  ([doc 41](41-districts-the-rest.md)).
- **Loud:** strongly punished (private army) — a desperate fallback.
- **Clever:** cut the estate's grid; work the rolling blackout
  ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)).
**Reward:** a major **High Ground** ending lever ([doc 04](04-story.md)) + a Heights
safehouse.

## EH8 — "Airshow" *(the Keys/airfield)*
**Target:** a private **airfield** during a charity airshow — Crane's getaway
aircraft and a cash-stuffed safe in the hangar.
- **Quiet:** ground-crew disguise; roll the plane out "for maintenance."
- **Loud:** seize the field; a runway chase.
- **Clever:** "buy" the plane with forged papers and simply fly it away.
**Reward:** an **aircraft** ([doc 27](27-vehicle-catalog.md)) + late-game traversal
freedom.

## Extended-heist design rules

- **Optional, never required** — these enrich; the campaign 9 stand alone
  ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)).
- **Each adds a *new wrinkle*** — underwater, mobile/rolling, storm-only,
  swap-not-steal, fly-away — so none reuses a campaign heist's lesson
  ([doc 07](07-missions.md)).
- **Discovered, not handed out** — found via contacts, rep, exploration, or world
  state ([doc 21](21-the-phone-and-meta-ui.md)).
- **Some carry choices** that feed faction rep and the endings
  ([doc 48](48-narrative-branching-system.md)).
- **Three approaches, always** ([doc 54](54-level-design-patterns.md)).
