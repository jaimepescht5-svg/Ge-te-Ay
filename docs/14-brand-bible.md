# 14 — Brand Bible

> Every brand in Port Soleil is **invented** so the world can be lived-in and
> mercilessly satirized without touching real IP (see
> [doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)). This is the master list. Names here are
> working drafts pending the clearance checklist before lock.

## How we use brands

- **Satire targets institutions, not real companies.** A predatory bank, a
  prosperity church, a gig app — *types*, exaggerated for comedy and theme.
- **Brands carry the theme.** The flood-and-money thesis shows up in advertising:
  flood insurance no one can afford, "luxury arcology" billboards, evacuation
  apps that are really data-harvesting.
- **Diegetic everywhere:** billboards, storefronts, radio ads, packaging, logos
  on vehicles, app icons on the in-game phone.

## Brand-management rules

- Run every brand through the **naming-collision checklist** ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md))
  before lock; log cleared names in the registry.
- Keep satire **punching up** at institutions and power, never at marginalized
  groups.
- Maintain visual distinctiveness: original logos, type, and color so nothing
  reads as a real-world mark.
