# 38 — Dynamic World Events & The City Calendar

> The systemic events that make Port Soleil feel like a place with a *life of its
> own* — the cyclical, scheduled, and reactive happenings that fill the open world
> between missions ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)). This is single-player's
> living calendar; the online seasonal version lives in
> [doc 30](30-post-launch-roadmap.md).

## Three layers of "stuff happening"

1. **Cyclical** — tied to time/tide/weather; predictable, learnable rhythms.
2. **Scheduled** — in-fiction calendar events (festivals, election, storm season).
3. **Reactive** — triggered by player actions and the faction war
   ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)).

## Layer 1 — Cyclical events (the daily/tidal pulse)

| Event | Trigger | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Dawn market** | Morning, the Cut/Mercado | Crowds, vendors, pickpocket & courier seeds ([doc 33](33-district-deepdive-the-cut.md)) |
| **Shift change** | Dusk, the Reach | Trucks, workers, a window for port jobs |
| **Club night** | Night, Downtown/Strip | Density, valets, drunk-driver seeds, deals |
| **King tide** | Tidal peak | Low blocks flood; boats needed; rescue seeds; smuggling channels open |
| **Low tide** | Tidal trough | Sunken caches exposed; foot shortcuts ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)) |
| **Heat wave** | Hot afternoons | Thin crowds, short tempers, more street seeds |

## Layer 2 — Scheduled calendar events (the city's year)

Port Soleil runs an in-fiction calendar the player learns and can exploit:

| Event | Vibe | Gameplay hooks |
|---|---|---|
| **Festival of Lights** | Street festival, parade, music | Parade-route heist windows, races, packed crowds to vanish in, radio event ([doc 13](13-radio-music.md)) |
| **Election season** | Satire, signage, rallies | The Reclamation escalates; Pelham missions; faction-funded "incidents" ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)) |
| **Storm season** | Dread builds | Evacuations, boarded shops, looting windows, weather-only jobs, thinned law ([doc 04](04-story.md)) |
| **Regatta / boat week** | Cayo Brava party | Sea races, smuggling cover, rich targets in the Keys |
| **Founders' Day** | The city mythologizes itself | Heritage tours (heist cover), Vega public appearances, irony |

> The **campaign's storm (Odette)** is the *climax* of storm season — the
> scheduled layer and the main story converge in Act III
> ([doc 35](35-mission-flow-and-pacing.md)).

## Layer 3 — Reactive events (the world answers you)

| Event | Triggered by | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Faction reprisal** | Hitting the Vegas/Crane hard | Ambushes, bounties, business attacks ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)) |
| **Turf flashpoint** | The background faction war | Open Vega-vs-Crane skirmishes you can tip |
| **Police crackdown** | High recent Heat in a district | Patrols, checkpoints, that district stays hot ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)) |
| **Business raid/audit** | Owning fronts + over-laundering | Defend-your-business missions ([doc 34](34-economy-balance.md)) |
| **Boyd closes in** | Story crimes / mistakes | The detective surfaces with a new lead ([doc 36](36-character-bios.md)) |
| **Copycat/streamer buzz** | Big spectacular crimes | Plumage reacts; Storm Chasers show up ([doc 21](21-the-phone-and-meta-ui.md)) |

## The storm as a mega-event (the showcase)

The hurricane is the most elaborate dynamic event — a multi-stage takeover of the
whole world ([doc 02](02-setting-world.md)):

1. **Watch** — Storm Watch radio ramps; sea state rises; far-off sky changes.
2. **Warning** — NPCs board up; voluntary evacuation; gas/store runs; prices spike.
3. **Mandatory evac** — roads jam then empty; **law thins**; looting windows open;
   weather-only missions appear.
4. **Landfall** — the set-piece: wind, surge floods the low map, **rolling
   blackouts** ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)), debris, downed lines; the world
   is transformed and dangerous.
5. **The eye** — eerie false calm; a beat to breathe or strike.
6. **Aftermath** — flooded districts, changed routes, rescue/recovery seeds, a
   raw and altered city.

Players learn to **weaponize** each stage (the thin law of evac, the cover of
landfall) — the storm is the ultimate "clever approach" enabler
([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)).

## Surfacing events to the player

- **Radio & talk shows** announce and react ([doc 37](37-radio-programming.md)).
- **The phone** pushes timely event notifications and map overlays
  ([doc 21](21-the-phone-and-meta-ui.md)) — opt-in, throttled, never nagging.
- **Environmental telegraphing** — the sky, the tide line, boarded windows — so
  events are *felt* before they're announced ([doc 15](15-art-direction.md)).

## Design rules for events

- **Learnable, not random:** cyclical/scheduled events reward players who learn the
  city's rhythms ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).
- **Events are opportunities, not interruptions:** they open play, never yank
  control away mid-mission ([doc 19](19-living-world-ai.md)).
- **Everything ties to theme:** the calendar keeps circling water, money, and
  power — the city's whole personality ([doc 24](24-world-lore-timeline.md)).
- **The director throttles** so events enrich without overwhelming
  ([doc 35](35-mission-flow-and-pacing.md)).
