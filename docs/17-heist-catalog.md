# 17 — Heist Catalog (The Marquee Scores)

> The ~9 major heists that spine the campaign, each built on the four-phase
> system ([doc 07](07-missions.md)) with **at least three viable approaches**
> (Quiet / Loud / Clever). Approaches below are sketches, not the only paths —
> the systems should permit hybrids and player invention.

---

## H1 — "The Sure Thing" *(Prologue — scripted)*
**Target:** A warehouse holding mixed Vega/Crane cash.
**Role:** Tutorial heist; teaches all three leads + the switch. Goes loud by
design. (Full beat sheet in [doc 16](16-vertical-slice-prologue.md).)
**Take:** The bag that starts everything. **Fallout:** two factions + Boyd on you.

---

## H2 — "Wash Day" *(Act I — first player-planned score)*
**Target:** A Vega-owned **24-Hour Wash** laundering hub skimming cartel cash.
- **Quiet:** Theo spoofs the books and you walk out the day's drop unseen.
- **Loud:** hit it on a cash-transfer Friday; fight off the Vega response.
- **Clever:** tip the cops to *one* end of the operation as a distraction, rob the
  other.
**Stakes/teaches:** introduces laundering, the fence, and faction Heat. **Reward:**
seed capital + the car wash becomes a buyable laundering front.

---

## H3 — "Open Water" *(Act I — the Keys)*
**Target:** A Cayo Brava **smuggling shipment** the Coast Guard is also hunting.
- **Quiet:** intercept at sea at night, swap the cargo buoys, vanish.
- **Loud:** cigarette-boat assault on the transfer; a running sea battle.
- **Clever:** pose as the buyers (Frankie's con) and walk the cargo off the dock.
**Teaches:** the **water network** and boats as primary tools. **Reward:** a
smuggling route business + the *Reaper* boat.

---

## H4 — "Repossession" *(Act II — the Reach)*
**Target:** A container at **Hialto port** holding Crane Coastal's off-book
hardware (surveillance gear tied to Theo's blacklist).
- **Quiet:** crane-operator disguise; move "your" container to a truck and drive
  out on forged manifests.
- **Loud:** breach the yard, fight through private security, smash-and-grab.
- **Clever:** start a small **dock-fire/HAZMAT scare** to trigger an evacuation,
  loot in the chaos.
**Teaches:** vertical/industrial set-piece, vehicle-as-tool (crane, semi).
**Reward:** Theo's personal intel cache; advances his arc.

---

## H5 — "The Gospel Cut" *(Act II — Sabal Springs)*
**Target:** The **Gospel of Gain** megachurch's count-room on a big-collection
Sunday (it launders for the Vegas).
- **Quiet:** join the congregation (Frankie's old flame is inside), slip to the
  count-room during the service.
- **Loud:** hit the armored pickup leaving the lot.
- **Clever:** con Pastor Goldwynn into moving the money early "for safety," then
  take it in transit.
**Teaches:** social engineering, comedy-heist tone, a personal-stakes
complication (Frankie). **Reward:** dirt on the Vega–church link (a story lever).

---

## H6 — "Highline" *(Act II — Marisol Heights)*
**Target:** A **Heights estate** vault during a Crane fundraiser; pure
infiltration on the only dry ground in the county.
- **Quiet:** the marquee stealth heist — disguises, hacked security, no shots.
- **Loud:** strongly punished here (private security + fast police) — a desperate
  fallback, not a plan.
- **Clever:** kill the power to the gated grid (a "rolling blackout") and work the
  dark.
**Teaches:** high-end stealth and that some places *demand* the quiet craft.
**Reward:** big clean payout + a Heights safehouse.

---

## H7 — "The Drawbridge Job" *(Act II climax — the betrayal heist)*
**Target:** An armored convoy moving Vega *and* Crane money across the iconic
**causeway drawbridge**.
- **Quiet:** rig the bridge controls (Theo) to trap the convoy mid-span; relieve
  it before backup crosses.
- **Loud:** the blockbuster set-piece — raise the bridge, box the convoy, fight on
  the span over open water.
- **Clever:** impersonate the escort (stolen security vehicles) and reroute the
  convoy into your hands.
**Teaches:** everything at once; this is where the **midpoint betrayal** lands
mid-execution (an ally flips / Boyd springs a trap). **Reward/Fallout:** the
double game collapses; you're now playing only for yourselves.

---

## H8 — "Eye of the Storm" *(Act III — penultimate)*
**Target:** With Hurricane Odette inbound, hit the **Vega organization's storm
reserve** as La Señora consolidates to flee.
- **Quiet:** move while the family evacuates; nearly impossible but flawless.
- **Loud:** open war on a half-empty, wind-battered Reach.
- **Clever:** use the **mandatory evacuation** itself — ride the chaos, let the
  storm be your cover and your getaway.
**Teaches:** the storm as a full gameplay layer (wind, surge, thinned law,
power-grid failures). **Reward:** the means to reach Crane; sets the finale fork.

---

## H9 — "The Ark Gala" *(Act III — the finale, branching)*
**Target:** **Sterling Crane** and the relief money, aboard **The Ark** the night
of landfall. (Sketched in [doc 07](07-missions.md).)
- **Quiet:** catering infiltration; crack the vault during the auction.
- **Loud:** storm the causeway approach; take the gala.
- **Clever:** fake the early-evac alarm; rob the Ark in the panic as the elite flee
  the very flood they engineered.
**Branches into the three endings** ([doc 04](04-story.md)): **Clean Break**
(take it and vanish), **Scorched Earth** (burn it all down), **High Ground**
(expose Crane, redirect the relief money, stay). Escape routes differ per
approach: **helipad, sea, or service tunnels** as the surge rises.

---

## Catalog design rules

- **No two heists teach the same lesson** — each adds a location type, a tool, or
  a tonal register the player hasn't met.
- **Personal stakes** thread through: at least three heists hinge on a lead's own
  arc (Theo/H4, Frankie/H5, Rae throughout the storm finale).
- **Approach economy:** Quiet = highest payout / lowest Heat / highest skill;
  Loud = reliable / expensive / hot; Clever = situational / spectacular / risky.
- **The world remembers every score** — completed heists leave lasting marks on
  district Heat, faction rep, and the news cycle.
