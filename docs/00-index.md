## The 60-second version

NEON DELTA is an **original** open-world crime saga in **Port Soleil**, a gorgeous
sinking coastal city. Play **three switchable outlaws** bound by a botched score,
pull **planned heists your way** (Quiet/Loud/Clever), and try to out-hustle a
cartel, a developer-billionaire, and a corrupt cop before a hurricane lands —
across a **living, drowning city** with a thesis. Legally distinct by design
([12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)); built responsibly ([20](20-tech-and-production.md));
anti-predatory online ([11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).

## The non-negotiable promises (the contract)

If we break these, we've failed regardless of anything else:
- **Original from frame one** ([12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).
- **No pay-to-win, ever** ([11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).
- **Accessibility is a launch pillar** ([23](23-accessibility.md)).
- **Punch up, never down** ([01](01-vision.md), [44](44-themes-and-meaning.md)).
- **Respect the player's time** — no grind gates ([10](10-economy-progression.md),
  [34](34-economy-balance.md)).
- **No crunch as a plan** ([20](20-tech-and-production.md)).
