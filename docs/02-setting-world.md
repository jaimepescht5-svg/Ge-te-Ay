# 02 — World & Setting

## Where: Port Soleil, Solano County

**Port Soleil** is a fictional metropolis on the **Sablon Coast** — an invented
stretch of warm, hurricane-prone Gulf shoreline in the fictional U.S. state of
**Verano**. It is a city built on reclaimed swamp and bad ideas: half glittering
glass towers and pastel deco, half cracked stilt-houses sinking back into the
brackish water that made room for them.

> **Tone reference, not a real place.** Port Soleil is a pastiche of warm-weather
> American port cities — the heat, the money, the music, the corruption — without
> being any one of them. No real city, brand, or person is depicted. See
> [doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md).

### The county at a glance

Solano County stretches from the dense urban core out across causeways and
swampland to small coastal towns, an industrial port, and the wild interior:

- **Port Soleil (the city)** — dense, vertical, neon, the heart of the map.
- **The Causeways** — long bridges over open water connecting the islands; pure
  driving theater.
- **Cayo Brava & the Keys** — sandbar islands, smuggling coves, the rich and the
  hidden.
- **The Glades / Bayou Verde** — swamp interior: airboats, hideouts, militias,
  alligator-farm fronts.
- **Hialto & the Industrial Reach** — the working port, container yards, refineries.
- **Sabal Springs** — faded inland suburb and strip-mall sprawl, the "real
  America" the postcards skip.

A full breakdown of districts lives in [doc 06](06-map-districts.md).

## When: the present, slightly heightened

A contemporary setting, turned up half a notch into satire. Smartphones, ride-
share, crypto bros, streamers, gig-economy everything, and a 24-hour disaster-
news cycle. The technology is familiar but every brand is invented, so the world
can be mocked freely.

## The central pressure: water and money

Two forces squeeze everyone in Solano County, and they drive the whole fiction:

1. **The water is coming.** Storms are stronger and the tide is higher every
   year. Whole blocks flood at king tide. This is the ticking clock under the
   city — and a literal, dynamic gameplay system (see Weather & Tide below).
2. **The money is leaving.** Out-of-state capital is buying the high ground,
   "revitalizing" neighborhoods, and pricing out everyone who built them.
   Development is the new gold rush, and it is filthy.

Crime, in Port Soleil, isn't an aberration from the system — it *is* the system,
just with worse lawyers. Our protagonists are amateurs in a city of professionals.

## Weather, tide & season (dynamic world system)

The environment is a live system, not set dressing:

- **Day/night cycle** with district-specific rhythms (markets at dawn, clubs at
  night, shift changes at the port).
- **Tide cycle** that floods low-lying streets and the Glades, opening or closing
  routes, hiding/revealing smuggling channels, and changing where you can drive
  vs. boat.
- **Storm system** culminating in named hurricanes — the season's climax. As a
  storm builds: skies turn, sea state rises, NPCs board up and evacuate, police
  thin out, looting opportunities open, and certain missions only exist in the
  chaos. The eye passing over is a set-piece.
- **Heat & humidity** affect crowd density and NPC behavior (more people out at
  dusk, fewer at noon).

These systems feed missions, the economy, and emergent play. A heist timed to
landfall is harder to escape but easier to get lost in.

## Culture & vibe

Port Soleil is loud, multilingual, and gorgeous. The fiction draws on a melting-
pot port culture: music spilling from every car, food trucks and supper clubs,
boxing gyms and megachurches, retirees and refugees and reality-TV hopefuls. The
satire targets **institutions** — predatory developers, grift-pastors,
wellness-cult influencers, privatized everything — while the **people** are
written with affection and specificity.

### Invented media & brands (sampler)

So the world can breathe without touching real IP:

- **Radio:** *Sablon FM* (Latin & soul), *Static 99* (storm-warped rock),
  *The Gospel of Gain* (talk/satire), *Brackish Beats* (electronic).
- **Soda & food:** *Solé Cola*, *Gator Bites*, *El Rey burgers*.
- **Tech & money:** *Vant谷 (Vantgo) rideshare*, *Hodl'em crypto*, *Plumage*
  social app, *NestEgg* predatory bank.
- **Megaprojects:** *The Ark* (a billionaire's flood-proof luxury arcology — a
  recurring antagonist symbol).

## The thesis in one line

**Port Soleil is paradise built on a sandbar — beautiful, doomed, and for sale —
and you're trying to grab your piece before the lights go out.**
