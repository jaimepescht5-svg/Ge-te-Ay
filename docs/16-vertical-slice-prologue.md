# 16 — Vertical Slice: The Prologue, Beat by Beat

> This is the playable opening — "The Sure Thing" — written as a vertical slice:
> the first 30–40 minutes that prove the game's voice, teach the three leads, and
> end on the hook that chains them together. Dialogue is direction, not final
> script.

## Goals of the prologue

1. **Teach the three verbs** — driving (Rae), stealth/hacking (Theo), combat
   (Frankie) — one per lead, organically.
2. **Introduce the switch** as the climactic skill.
3. **Sell the tone** — sincere pulp, satire + heart — in the first ten minutes.
4. **End on the hook:** one bag of money, two factions, a detective's photo, three
   strangers welded together.

## Cold open — "Postcard from the Bottom"

- **Black screen, radio static, then Storm Watch 24:** "...Tropical Depression
  Nine has strengthened. The county urges residents *not* to panic..." Cut to:
- **A drone-glide over Port Soleil at golden hour** — causeway, towers, the Cut's
  pastel rows, the Glades, the Ark rising on the skyline. Gorgeous. The city's
  theme plays. A billboard: *The Ark — Rise above it all.*
- **Hard cut** to a cramped motel room in Sabal Springs. Sweat. A box fan. Frankie
  Delgado counting cash he doesn't have. **Title card: NEON DELTA.**

## Beat 1 — Frankie pitches the score *(intro: voice & tone)*
- Playable Frankie, on foot, walking a Sabal Springs strip mall to meet Rae at a
  diner. **Teaches:** movement, interaction, the phone, the world's texture
  (satirical billboards, NPC barks, a Vantgo driver complaining about pay).
- **Diner scene (cutscene):** Frankie sells the "clean" warehouse job to a
  skeptical Rae. Establishes their history and her reluctance. *"Last time you
  said clean, I did eighteen months of physical therapy and you sent a fruit
  basket."* — *"It was a* really *nice basket."*

## Beat 2 — Rae drives *(teach: driving + Redline)*
- Switch to **Rae** for the first time (a gentle, guided switch tutorial). Drive
  Frankie across the Cut to pick up the third member.
- **Teaches:** core driving, the handbrake, traffic, the mini-map, and a first
  taste of **Redline** when a light run forces a near-miss (slow-mo, the hook of
  her power). Optional: a tiny street-race side-temptation to show the world is
  open.
- Banter establishes Rae's father, the garage, and the developers circling it.

## Beat 3 — Recruiting Theo *(teach: stealth/recon)*
- They find **Theo** mid-job, casing the warehouse from a rooftop. Switch to
  **Theo**.
- **Teaches:** crouch/stealth, **Overwatch** tagging (mark the guards, cameras,
  exits below), and the quiet-vs-loud framing. Theo's read of the building *is*
  the heist's Phase 1 in miniature.
- **Reveal to the player (not the crew):** Theo tags a crate marked with a Vega
  sigil *and* a Crane Coastal seizure notice. The "clean" job is anything but.

## Beat 4 — The job goes loud *(teach: combat + the switch)*
- The plan: in quiet, grab the bag, out clean. It survives about ninety seconds.
- A guard makes Frankie; an alarm trips. **Switch to Frankie** for the first
  combat — **teaches:** cover, shooting, **Hot Streak**, takedowns.
- Then the **set-piece**: the player must rotate all three under fire —
  - **Theo** on a hacked camera/door, opening the path and tagging threats;
  - **Frankie** holding the floor;
  - **Rae** bringing the car around / driving the escape.
- This is the **switch tutorial as climax**: "you only survive together," taught
  by making it literally true.

## Beat 5 — The chase *(payoff: driving under fire)*
- Back to **Rae** driving, Frankie on the guns, Theo navigating via tapped traffic
  cams. A chase through the Cut and onto a **causeway at dusk** — the first
  signature vista as a set-piece. **Teaches:** combat-driving, losing Heat (first
  taste of the pursuit system), and switching mid-chase.
- They lose the cops by ditching into a canal boat — first hint of the **water
  network**.

## Beat 6 — The hook *(the welding)*
- Safehouse, night. They open the bag: **far more money than promised**, plus
  documents proving it's both Vega cartel cash and Crane's earmarked "relief"
  funds. They just robbed the two most powerful forces in the county at once.
- **Intercut:** Det. **Boyd** at the scene, pulling a **security still of all
  three faces**. He smiles. *"Well, hello."*
- **Final scene:** the three argue — split up vs. stick together. Rae: *"We don't
  know each other."* Frankie: *"We do now. That's the problem."* Theo, quiet:
  *"Two armies want us dead and a cop has our faces. Apart, we're three easy
  funerals. Together, maybe we're a problem they can't solve."*
- **Smash to title / Act I.** The crew exists.

## What the slice must prove (review gate)

- [ ] The three leads feel **distinct in the hand**, not just the cutscene.
- [ ] The **switch** feels like the game's best idea by the end.
- [ ] A player can **describe a moment** they made (a Redline near-miss, a clutch
      switch) — emergent ownership in 40 minutes.
- [ ] Tone lands: people **laughed** *and* felt the dread of the storm/flood.
- [ ] The city looked like **nowhere else** — the art identity reads instantly.
