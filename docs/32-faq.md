# 32 — FAQ

> Anticipated questions — from players, press, and stakeholders — with answers
> grounded in the rest of the bible. A quick way to pressure-test that the design
> is coherent and the promises are kept.

## About the game

**Q: What is NEON DELTA?**
A third-person, open-world action-crime game set in the fictional sinking coastal
city of **Port Soleil**. Drive, scheme, shoot, and pull heists as **three
switchable outlaws** across a living county, while the tide rises and a hurricane
closes in ([doc 01](01-vision.md), [doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)).

**Q: Is this a GTA clone?**
No. It's an **original game in a shared genre.** Open worlds, driving, shooting,
and crime sandboxes are genre conventions no one owns; every *specific* thing —
the city, the characters, the story, the brands, the look, the music — is
original ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)). Inspired by a genre, copying no
product.

**Q: What makes it different?**
The **switch** built into the whole story and heist play; a **living, drowning
city** where tide and storms are real systems; **Quiet/Loud/Clever** heists that
are mechanically and economically distinct; **sincere-pulp** tone (mock
institutions, love people); and a **public anti-predatory monetization charter**
([doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)).

## Story & characters

**Q: Who do I play?**
Three leads you switch between: **Rae** (ex-stunt driver), **Theo** (burned
hacker), and **Frankie** (motor-mouth grifter), welded together by one botched
score ([doc 03](03-characters.md)).

**Q: How long is the campaign?**
~30–40 hours main story, plus a deep web of side stories, hustles, and an evolving
world ([doc 04](04-story.md), [doc 18](18-side-stories.md)).

**Q: Are there multiple endings?**
Yes — three core endings (**Clean Break / Scorched Earth / High Ground**) plus a
**personal coda** for each lead, driven by your choices ([doc 04](04-story.md)).

## Gameplay

**Q: Do I have to play violently?**
No. Many situations resolve through **driving, stealth, hacking, talking, or
cleverness.** There's a full **non-lethal arsenal** and a viable pacifist path;
the smartest play is rarely the loudest ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md),
[doc 28](28-weapon-catalog.md)).

**Q: How do heists work?**
A four-phase system — **Setup, Planning, Execution, Escape** — with multiple
viable approaches, real prep tradeoffs, mid-job improvisation, and lasting
consequences ([doc 07](07-missions.md), [doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)).

**Q: What's the "Heat" system?**
A **legible, escalating pursuit** model: cops search your *last known position*,
never teleport, and you lose them with skill — break sightlines, change vehicles
and clothes, blend in ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)).

**Q: Is there water/flying gameplay?**
Yes — boats are a **co-equal road network** (half the county is water), airboats
rule the swamp, and earned aircraft open late-game freedom
([doc 08](08-vehicles.md), [doc 27](27-vehicle-catalog.md)).

## Online & monetization

**Q: Is there multiplayer?**
Yes — **Solano Online**, a persistent shared Port Soleil for co-op heists, crews,
and businesses. It's a **parallel experience, never a tax on the campaign**
([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).

**Q: Is there pay-to-win or loot boxes?**
**No.** The monetization charter is a hard pillar: cosmetics/convenience only,
nothing buyable grants power, no randomized paid loot, no FOMO grind, everything
buyable is also earnable ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md),
[doc 30](30-post-launch-roadmap.md)).

**Q: Do I need to play online to finish the game?**
No. The single-player campaign is **complete and self-contained**; online is
never required ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).

## Accessibility & content

**Q: How accessible is it?**
Accessibility is a **launch pillar** — per-system difficulty, full remapping,
scalable HUD/subtitles, colorblind-safe (no color-only info), captioned sound
events, cognitive/navigation aids, and a content-sensitivity menu
([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)).

**Q: How mature is the content?**
**Adult-rated** — crime, violence, satire, strong language — but the edge comes
from **character and stakes, not cruelty or shock**, and a content slider lets you
soften specifics ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md), [doc 23](23-accessibility.md)).

**Q: Does the satire punch down?**
No. We satirize **institutions and power** (developers, grift-churches, gig apps),
never marginalized groups; the *people* are written with affection
([doc 01](01-vision.md), [doc 14](14-brand-bible.md)).

## Post-launch

**Q: What's the post-launch plan?**
Stability/accessibility first, then **two substantial story expansions** and
**free-core seasonal updates** for online that *evolve the city's continuity* —
content, not paywalls ([doc 30](30-post-launch-roadmap.md)).

**Q: Will the world change over time?**
Yes — the flood rises, factions war, districts change. Seasons are **chapters in
one ongoing story**, not disconnected themes ([doc 30](30-post-launch-roadmap.md)).

## Production

**Q: How is it being built responsibly?**
With **scope discipline** (vertical slice → one district → scale), **no crunch as
a plan**, embedded accessibility, and legal/originality review gates
([doc 20](20-tech-and-production.md), [doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).
