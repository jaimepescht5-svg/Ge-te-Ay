# 43 — Achievements & 100% Completion

> The achievement/trophy list and the "100%" completion definition. These shape
> *how players are nudged to experience the game* — so they're a design tool, not
> an afterthought. Our rule: **reward exploration, mastery, and the things that
> make the best stories — never tedium or grind** ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).

## Philosophy

- **Celebrate, don't chore.** Achievements mark memorable accomplishments and
  encourage trying systems players might skip — not "collect 500 widgets."
- **No grind, no luck, no pay.** Nothing gated behind tedium, RNG, or purchases
  ([doc 11](11-online-multiplayer.md)).
- **Approach-agnostic.** Story achievements never force a single playstyle; where
  one rewards Quiet, another rewards Loud — both are celebrated.
- **Accessible.** Achievable on any difficulty/assist setting
  ([doc 23](23-accessibility.md)); no "hardest-mode-only" walls.

## Story achievements (the spine)

One per major beat — completion markers, not skill walls:
- **"The Sure Thing"** — finish the prologue.
- **"Small Fish"**, **"Crossfire"**, **"Landfall"** — complete each Act.
- **"Clean Break" / "Scorched Earth" / "High Ground"** — reach each ending.
- **"Three Funerals Avoided"** — complete all three leads' personal codas
  ([doc 04](04-story.md)).

## Heist mastery achievements (encourage approach variety)

Tied to the catalog ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)):
- **"Whisper"** — complete any heist fully Quiet (no kills, no alarms).
- **"Bang"** — complete any heist Loud and escape without losing the take.
- **"Magician"** — complete any heist via the Clever approach.
- **"The Full Toolbox"** — complete the same heist all three ways (across saves).
- **"Storm Robber"** — pull a score during landfall ([doc 38](38-dynamic-events-calendar.md)).
- **"Greedy"** — grab every optional objective on a single heist.

## 100% completion checklist (the in-game tracker)

A transparent, *finite*, **non-grindy** list — players always see what's left and
why it's worth doing:

- [ ] All **story missions** + all three **personal codas**.
- [ ] All **side-story arcs** ([doc 18](18-side-stories.md)).
- [ ] All **Strangers** (the anthology vignettes).
- [ ] Each **heist** completed at least once (extra: all-three-ways tracked
      separately, not required for 100%).
- [ ] All **collectibles**: murals, tide-line shrines, sunken caches, pirate
      signal.
- [ ] All **activities** sampled: race series, stunt jumps, each hobby once.
- [ ] **Own** at least one business and one safehouse.
- [ ] Reach **one** ending (replays unlock the others; 100% never demands all
      three in one save).

> **Explicitly excluded from 100%:** "do X 100 times" grinds, RNG drops, and
> anything online-gated. 100% is a **tour of the best of the game**, not a chore
> list ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).

## Completion reward

- A **New Game+** unlock, a unique cosmetic/vehicle (the restored *Saint* livery),
  and a reflective epilogue scene — a thank-you, not a carrot
  ([doc 30](30-post-launch-roadmap.md)).

## Achievement design rules

- **Each achievement points at something worth doing** — a system to try, a place
  to find, a story to finish.
- **Never punish a playstyle** — pair every "do it quiet" with a "do it loud."
- **Discoverable & fair** — no missables without warning; chapter-select/replay
  lets players mop up ([doc 35](35-mission-flow-and-pacing.md)).
- **Original cheeky voice** in the naming — the achievements are characters too.
