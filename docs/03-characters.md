# 03 — Characters

## The crew (three playable protagonists)

The campaign is told through three switchable leads. Each has a distinct
silhouette, voice, skill identity, social network, and arc. They are not class
archetypes — they're people who happen to be good at different felonies.

---

### Rae Castellano — *The Wheel*
**~38. Ex-stunt driver. The closest thing the crew has to a conscience.**

Rae spent fifteen years as Hollywood's go-to precision driver until a stunt went
wrong, someone died, and the studios stopped calling. She came home to Port
Soleil to look after her ailing father and found the city she left is being
bulldozed into condos. She drives for hire now — sometimes couriers, sometimes
worse. She's the most competent and the most reluctant; the one who keeps a
ledger of what they've done.

- **Signature:** *Redline* — a focus state that slows time and perfects vehicle
  handling for daring escapes and stunts.
- **Plays like:** the getaway and the chase. Best driver, best under pressure,
  reads traffic like a chessboard.
- **Arc:** from running *from* her past to choosing what she's willing to become.
- **Heart:** her father, Tomás, and the old garage the developers want.

---

### Theo "Ghost" Okafor — *The Key*
**~31. Burned intelligence contractor. Allergic to being seen.**

Theo did three years of off-books surveillance and network work for a private
intelligence firm before he learned what the data was being used for and walked —
straight onto a blacklist. Now he's a ghost with no employer and a lot of
enemies who'd prefer he stay dead. Quiet, exacting, dryly funny, and quietly
furious at a world that turned his talents into a weapon.

- **Signature:** *Overwatch* — tag cameras, phones, drones, and people; see
  through walls briefly; remote-hack devices.
- **Plays like:** the infiltration and the prep. Best stealth, hacking, recon,
  and gadgets. Turns heists from gunfights into clockwork.
- **Arc:** from hiding to deciding the only way to be free is to be loud, once.
- **Heart:** his sister, Ada, whose safety the blacklist threatens.

---

### Frankie "Sticks" Delgado — *The Mouth*
**~26. Small-time grifter. Big-time optimist. Terrible at math.**

Frankie owes the wrong people because Frankie always owes the wrong people. A
charming, motormouthed hustler who has talked his way into and out of more
trouble than the other two combined — and whose latest "sure thing" is the
botched score that chains all three together. Reckless, loyal, funny, and
desperate to finally be the guy who *wins*.

- **Signature:** *Hot Streak* — adrenaline/aggression state: faster reload, melee
  takedowns, and a social "fast-talk" that defuses or manipulates NPCs.
- **Plays like:** the loud option and the people work. Best close-quarters,
  improvisation, social engineering, and street connections.
- **Arc:** from chasing the score to learning what's actually worth keeping.
- **Heart:** the crew itself — they're the family he keeps almost getting killed.

---

## How the trio works

- **The botched score** (the prologue) makes each of them indispensable to the
  others: Rae can't get out without Theo's access; Theo can't move without
  Frankie's connections; Frankie can't survive his debt without the money only
  all three can take.
- **Tension is the engine.** Rae wants out clean, Theo wants it surgical, Frankie
  wants it *now*. Mission planning surfaces these conflicts as real choices.
- **Switching** is encouraged everywhere — each lead is doing something during
  the others' downtime (Rae fixing the garage, Theo running surveillance, Frankie
  digging a new hole). Switch-in vignettes keep them alive between missions.

---

## Principal antagonists

### Lourdes "La Señora" Vega — the established order
Matriarch of the **Vega organization**, the old-money crime family that has run
Port Soleil's ports and politics for thirty years. Elegant, patient, genuinely
believes she's the only thing standing between the city and chaos. Not a monster
— a CEO. The crew's botched score hit *her* money.

### Sterling Crane — the new order
Out-of-state developer-billionaire behind **The Ark** arcology and a campaign to
"reclaim" the coast. Buys cops, councils, and weather data. Sees the coming flood
not as a catastrophe but as a *liquidation event* — a chance to own everything
once the current owners drown. The real apex predator; the Vegas are small-time
next to him.

### Det. Marcus "Hound" Boyd — the law that isn't
A homicide detective with a closing rate built on planted evidence and a side
income from whoever's paying. Obsessed with the crew after the botched score
embarrasses him. The face of a department that is just another gang with better
PR.

## Supporting cast (sampler)

- **Tomás Castellano** — Rae's father; a retired mechanic; the crew's reluctant
  fixer and moral center.
- **Ada Okafor** — Theo's sister; an ER nurse who keeps stitching the crew back
  together and hating herself for it.
- **"Mama" Yolette** — runs a Glades supper club that fronts a smuggling network;
  the crew's first real ally.
- **Donnie Pelham** — disgraced city councilman turned the crew's inside man,
  always one favor from flipping.
- **Iris** — Theo's AI-assisted tool persona / contact handler in the UI (an
  in-fiction interface, not a real-brand assistant).
