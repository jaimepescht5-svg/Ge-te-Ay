# 31 — Announce Trailer Script

> A shot-by-shot **reveal trailer** (~90 seconds) — the first public impression of
> NEON DELTA. Goal: sell the **city, the tone, the three leads, the switch, and
> the storm** in ninety seconds, and end on a logo nobody confuses for anything
> else ([doc 26](26-pitch-and-positioning.md)). Music-led, dialogue-sparse.

---

## Pre-title (0:00–0:35) — "The Postcard"

**0:00** — Black. The crackle of a radio tuning. A weather voice, calm, distant:
*"...Tropical system Odette continues to strengthen in the gulf..."* It fades
under a slow, warm bassline (Velvet Hour energy, [doc 13](13-radio-music.md)).

**0:05** — **WIDE, golden hour.** A drone glide over **Port Soleil** — the
causeway glittering, pastel rows of the Cut, glass towers, the swamp, **The Ark**
rising on the skyline. Gorgeous. A billboard slides past: *Rise above it all.*
*(This is the bait: it looks like paradise.)*

**0:18** — Quick warm cuts, no action yet — the **life** of the city: café-cubano
steam, kids on bikes through a flooded street, a supper-club band, an old man
watching the tide creep up his steps. **Establish what's worth losing.**

**0:28** — The water laps a *little* higher up that step. The music holds a beat
too long. A subtle wrongness. **Cut to black on the downbeat.**

---

## Title sting (0:35–0:40)

**0:35** — Neon flickers to life in the dark, spelling it out, salt-corroded and
electric:

> **NEON DELTA**

**0:38** — Under it, small: *Welcome to Port Soleil.* The neon buzzes, half a
letter dying. **Snap to black.**

---

## The turn (0:40–1:05) — "The Sure Thing goes wrong"

**0:40** — Hard cut. **The motel room.** Frankie, mid-pitch, grinning at someone
off-camera: *"It's a* sure thing." Beat. Smash to **the warehouse alarm** — red
light, everyone moving. *(Tone flips from postcard to pulp.)*

**0:46** — Rapid-fire **character intros via action**, one verb each:
- **RAE** — slams a muscle car into a reverse-180, dust and neon, eyes cold.
  *Redline* time-smear flickers. *(The wheel.)*
- **THEO** — on a rooftop, fingers on a tablet; below him, guards and cameras
  light up with tags; a door clicks open. *(The key.)*
- **FRANKIE** — vaults a counter mid-sentence, fist swinging, laughing.
  *(The mouth.)*

**0:58** — **The switch, shown plainly:** mid-firefight, the camera *whips* from
Frankie pinned behind cover → up to Theo lining a shot → out to Rae skidding the
getaway car to the door. Three angles, one second of game time. *(The hook.)*

---

## The world & the stakes (1:05–1:25)

**1:05** — A montage cut to the bassline's build — **breadth**: a causeway chase
at king tide, waves over the deck; an **airboat** ripping through the Glades; a
**seaplane** lifting off a flooded avenue; a black-tie infiltration of a Heights
gala; a boat slipping past the Keys at night.

**1:14** — Three faces of power flash by — **La Señora** at a long table,
**Sterling Crane** smiling at a model of The Ark, **Boyd** holding a photo of the
three leads. *(Who wants them dead.)*

**1:18** — The sky **turns.** A green-grey wall of cloud. NPCs boarding windows.
The radio distorts into an emergency tone. **The storm.** Rae, quiet, over the
chaos: *"This is our landfall."*

**1:22** — One last image: the three of them, backs to the camera, walking toward
the storm-lit city. Small. Together.

---

## End title (1:25–1:35)

**1:25** — Snap to black. The neon logo again, fuller this time:

> **NEON DELTA**
> *Salt and neon.*

**1:30** — Below, clean: platform / studio / "Wishlist now." A final radio crackle
— *"...residents are urged not to panic..."* — cut hard to silence.

**1:34** — One last beat of black, then a single line of text, dry, the game's
whole soul:

> *Paradise is for sale. Get yours before the lights go out.*

**END.**

---

## Trailer design rules

- **Sell the city first, the violence second** — the postcard-then-turn structure
  makes the stakes *mean* something ([doc 02](02-setting-world.md)).
- **Show the switch as gameplay,** unmistakably — it's the signature
  ([doc 05](05-gameplay-mechanics.md)).
- **Three distinct silhouettes** so viewers leave knowing there are *three* of
  them and who they are ([doc 03](03-characters.md), [doc 15](15-art-direction.md)).
- **The storm is the closer** — it's the ticking clock and the emotional key
  ([doc 04](04-story.md)).
- **Original from frame one** — no shot, font, or beat evokes a specific existing
  game; the logo and tagline are wholly ours ([doc 12](12-legal-distinctiveness.md)).
- **No gameplay we can't ship** — the trailer is a promise, not a lie.
