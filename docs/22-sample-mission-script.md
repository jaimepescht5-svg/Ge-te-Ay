# 22 — Sample Mission Script: "Wash Day"

> A full scripted walkthrough of **H2 — "Wash Day"** ([doc 17](17-heist-catalog.md)),
> the first player-planned score, written to demonstrate the game's **voice,
> pacing, and how systems + story interlock**. Dialogue is direction-level, not
> final. `[SYSTEM]` notes mark where mechanics drive the scene.

---

## Cold open — The Garage, night

*Tomás's garage in the Cut. Rain ticking on a tin roof. The three leads around a
workbench covered in printouts. `[SYSTEM] Planning Board UI opens.]`*

**FRANKIE:** Okay, okay, hear me out. The 24-Hour Wash off Marlin. It's not a car
wash. I mean it *is* a car wash, but it's, like, *spiritually* a bank. Vega money
goes in dirty, comes out smelling like spring rain.

**RAE:** And you know this how.

**FRANKIE:** I used to date the assistant manager. We parted amicably.

**THEO:** *(not looking up from a laptop)* You parted owing her four hundred
dollars.

**FRANKIE:** *Amicably.*

**RAE:** *(to Theo)* Is it real?

**THEO:** *(turns the laptop around — security feed of the wash)* It's real. Cash
drop every evening, armored pickup Friday. Cameras are a decade old. Their network
is a toaster with a password taped to it.

**TOMÁS:** *(from across the room, wiping his hands)* You rob the Vegas a second
time, they stop thinking you're lucky and start thinking you're a problem.

**FRANKIE:** We're *already* a problem, Tomás. Might as well get paid for it.

*Tomás just looks at Rae. She holds his eyes a second, then looks away —
`[SYSTEM] Relationship beat: Tomás standing −, Rae notes it.]`*

**RAE:** *(quiet)* We need the money. We do it clean and we do it once.

---

## Planning — The Board

`[SYSTEM] Approach select. Three lanes light up.]`

**THEO (V.O., as the player hovers each):**
- **Quiet:** "I forge the day's ledger. We walk the drop out the back. Nobody
  knows it's gone till the audit. Best money, no heat — if your hands are steady."
- **Loud:** "Friday. The armored pickup. More cash, but we're fighting Vega
  muscle on a public street. Bring friends and bandages."
- **Clever:** "We tip Boyd's people to the *front* operation. Cops raid the
  lobby, we clean out the count-room while everyone's face-down on the tile."

*The script below follows the **Quiet** path; the others are authored in
parallel. Player choice is real ([doc 07](07-missions.md)).*

`[SYSTEM] Crew & gear: hire a driver or let Rae wheel; buy suppressors / a
staff disguise. Budget tradeoffs shown.]`

**FRANKIE:** I vote quiet. Quiet means nobody gets shot.

**RAE:** Quiet means *nobody gets shot if nobody screws up.*

**FRANKIE:** ...I vote quiet *tentatively.*

---

## Travel — Drive to the Wash

`[SYSTEM] Switch to Rae driving. Free traversal; Velvet Hour on the radio
([doc 13](13-radio-music.md)). Tide is high — Marlin Ave is ankle-deep,
handling shifts ([doc 08](08-vehicles.md)).]`

**THEO (phone):** Streets are flooding on the south approach. Tide's not on the
schedule we planned for.

**RAE:** Then we park north and walk the last block. *(beat)* Welcome to Soleil.
Even the water's on Vega's payroll.

---

## Infiltration — Switch to Theo

`[SYSTEM] Switch to Theo. Overwatch available: tag two attendants, one camera,
the office door, the count-room.]`

**THEO (V.O.):** Two on the floor, one in the office. Camera covers the bay, not
the back hall. Their "vault" is a door a strong opinion could open.

`[SYSTEM] Player hacks the camera loop and the office lock. Frankie, in a staff
coverall, walks the floor as the distraction — switch to Frankie.]`

**FRANKIE:** *(to an attendant, loud, charming)* Hey — hey, man, you do
undercarriage? I got, like, a *swamp* under there. Drove through the Glades, came
out the other side basically a houseboat —

*`[SYSTEM] Fast-talk holds the attendant's attention while Theo moves. A meter
fills. If it empties — the attendant turns — the alarm risk spikes.]`*

**ATTENDANT:** We close in ten, man.

**FRANKIE:** Ten minutes is *plenty* of time for a miracle. Ask my priest.

---

## The Wrinkle — improvise

`[SYSTEM] Scripted deviation: the office manager returns early. Player must
adapt. Options surface dynamically — hide (Theo), intercept-and-charm (Frankie),
or abort.]`

**THEO (whisper, comms):** Frankie. Manager. Coming your way. *Do the thing.*

**FRANKIE:** *(spinning to the manager, arms wide)* MARISOL. Tell me you still
work here. Tell me the universe is kind.

**MANAGER (Marisol):** ...You owe me four hundred dollars.

**FRANKIE:** And I'm here to PAY it. With *interest.* In *feelings.*

*`[SYSTEM] A new micro-choice: pay her off (cash), talk past her (fast-talk
check), or Theo slips out behind her while she's distracted. Player picks.]`*

---

## Escape — beat the Heat

`[SYSTEM] Money secured. The manager hits a silent alarm offscreen if mishandled
→ Heat tier 2. Switch to Rae for the getaway; flooded streets change the chase
([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)).]`

**RAE:** *(as they pile in)* Clean, he said. *Once,* he said.

**FRANKIE:** It WAS clean! Define clean! Nobody's bleeding!

**THEO:** A Vega counting-house just lost its day. By morning, La Señora knows our
work has a *style.* That's not nothing.

*`[SYSTEM] Lose the tail via a canal-boat switch — first reuse of the water
escape from the prologue. Heat clears at the safehouse.]`*

---

## Aftermath — The Garage, dawn

*The take on the workbench. `[SYSTEM] Split & launder UI: the car wash they just
robbed can be BOUGHT as a laundering front ([doc 10](10-economy-progression.md)).
Irony noted on-screen.]`*

**FRANKIE:** So we buy the place we just robbed, and launder the money we stole,
*through* it.

**THEO:** Yes.

**FRANKIE:** *(genuine awe)* This is the most beautiful thing I've ever heard.

**RAE:** *(counting, not smiling)* It's a start. *(looks at the door Tomás left
through)* ...He's not wrong, you know. About the second time.

*Hold on Rae. The city's theme creeps in. `[SYSTEM] Faction rep: Vega −,
Streetcred +. New business available. Boyd gains a lead — a future complication
flagged.]`*

**CUT TO BLACK.**

---

## What this script demonstrates (review notes)

- **Voice:** Frankie (motor-mouth heart), Theo (deadpan precision), Rae
  (reluctant conscience) are distinct on the page alone ([doc 03](03-characters.md)).
- **Systems-in-fiction:** planning lanes, the switch, Overwatch, fast-talk, tide,
  Heat, laundering, faction rep — every mechanic arrives *through* character.
- **Fail-forward:** the wrinkle turns a plan-break into the funniest, most
  characterful beat ([doc 07](07-missions.md)).
- **Theme:** "even the water's on Vega's payroll," and buying-the-thing-you-robbed
  — the flood-and-money thesis in a single side score.
- **Consequence:** the world *remembers* (rep shifts, Boyd advances, Tomás's
  warning lands) — nothing is consequence-free.
