## Part B — Port Soleil street slang (in-world)

> Used by NPCs, the leads, and the radio. Built from a port-culture, multilingual,
> swamp-and-neon melting pot. **All invented** — evocative, not borrowed from a
> specific real dialect.

### People & roles
- **A wheel** — a getaway/precision driver (Rae is *"the best wheel on the coast"*).
- **A key** — an infiltrator/hacker who opens doors (Theo).
- **A mouth** — a talker/con artist/face (Frankie).
- **Dry money** — out-of-state wealth buying the high ground (Crane's people).
- **Salt** — a true local; born below the flood line. *"She's salt — she'll never
  sell."*
- **A barnacle** — someone who won't leave a sinking neighborhood (affectionate or
  insulting depending on tone).
- **Gala rats** — the rich who summer in the Heights and the Keys.
- **A badge with a price** — a bought cop (i.e., most of them).

### Places & geography
- **The Cut** — Old Soleil (also the act of getting cut out of your home by
  developers — the double meaning is intentional).
- **Going to the Glades** — disappearing; lying low. *"He went to the Glades"* can
  mean hiding *or* dead in the swamp.
- **The dry side** — Marisol Heights; by extension, anyone who's "made it."
- **King tide** — the highest flood of the cycle; also slang for "everything going
  wrong at once."
- **The high ground** — safety, money, or moral superiority, usually ironic.

### Crime & money
- **A wash** — laundering (and the literal car-wash fronts). *"Run it through a
  wash."*
- **Spring rain** — clean money (from the car-wash front's "smell"). *"Came out
  smelling like spring rain."* ([doc 22](22-sample-mission-script.md))
- **A sure thing** — a job that will absolutely go wrong (Frankie's signature
  phrase; the prologue's name — pure dramatic irony).
- **Going loud / staying quiet** — the heist approach choice, now everyday slang
  for any confrontation.
- **Soaked** — broke, ruined, or caught. *"If we miss the tide we're soaked."*
- **Heat's on / Heat's local** — pursuit status ([doc 09](09-weapons-combat.md)).
- **A drumbeat** — a recurring payoff/protection payment.

### Weather & the flood (the city's obsession)
- **She's turning** — a storm is forming offshore (always *she*).
- **Boarding up** — both literal storm-prep and "getting ready for the worst."
- **The eye** — a false calm before things get worse; betrayal weather.
- **Landfall** — the climax of anything. *"This is our landfall, boys."*
- **High and dry** — the dream and the lie: safe above the water (nobody really
  is).

### Interjections & color
- **"Sun's out."** — the coast is clear / it's safe.
- **"Mind the tide."** — be careful; watch your timing.
- **"That's Soleil."** — a shrug at the city's beautiful corruption (the world's
  catchphrase; cf. Rae in [doc 22](22-sample-mission-script.md)).
- **"Salt and neon."** — the city itself; what it's made of.

## Usage rules for writers

- **Slang earns its place** — a little goes a long way; never wall-of-dialect.
- **Leads have signature phrases** (Frankie: *"sure thing," "define clean"*;
  Rae: *"that's Soleil"*; Theo: dry literalism) for instant voice
  ([doc 03](03-characters.md)).
- **Double meanings carry theme** — *the Cut*, *dry money*, *landfall* all do
  double duty (place + the flood-and-money thesis).
- **Keep it invented and respectful** — flavor a fictional melting pot; never
  caricature a real community ([doc 01](01-vision.md)).
